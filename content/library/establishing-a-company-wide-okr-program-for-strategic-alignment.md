# Establishing a Company-Wide OKR Program for Strategic Alignment

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

Objective and Key Results (OKRs) are essential for driving focus and accountability in any organization. Jurnii partnered with a client to design and implement a comprehensive OKR program that aligned company goals with functional objectives, fostering a culture of transparency and continuous improvement.

## The Challenge

Aligning Objectives Across a Complex Organization

The client faced significant challenges in their goal-setting process:

- Lack of alignment between company-wide objectives and functional team goals.
- Inconsistent tracking and reporting mechanisms for key metrics.
- Limited collaboration on setting and reviewing objectives, resulting in misaligned priorities.

To overcome these hurdles, the client needed a structured OKR framework that unified the organization under shared goals while empowering teams to take ownership of their metrics.

## The Solution

A Tailored OKR Mapping Framework

Jurnii deployed a collaborative and systematic approach to create a company-wide OKR program that delivered measurable results.

### 1\. Insight-Driven Workshops

We conducted a series of workshops to:

- **Review research and insights** on the client’s brand and market position, ensuring OKRs reflected strategic priorities.
- **Co-create OKRs** at both the company and functional levels through voting and alignment exercises.
- **Integrate OKRs** with the existing measurement framework to link objectives to tangible metrics.

### 2\. Quarterly Progress Reviews

To foster accountability and adaptability, we established a cadence of quarterly reviews to:

- Track progress against OKRs.
- Identify areas for improvement.
- Ensure alignment with evolving business priorities.

## The Outcome

A Robust and Scalable OKR Program

Jurnii’s collaboration resulted in a structured, actionable OKR framework that drove alignment and accountability across the client’s organization. Key achievements include:

1.  **Populated OKR Matrix**: A comprehensive matrix linking company-wide goals with functional objectives, ensuring every team’s efforts supported the broader strategy.
2.  **Ownership and Accountability**: Clear ownership of metrics was established, empowering teams to drive their objectives and track outcomes effectively.
3.  **Reporting Template**: A standardized template was created to monitor progress and report on targets, enhancing visibility and decision-making.
4.  **Company-Wide Forum**: A recurring forum was introduced to review OKRs each quarter, fostering collaboration and a culture of continuous improvement.
