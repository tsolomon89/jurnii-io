# Automating the Radar: Building a Modern Competitive Intelligence Unit in iGaming

By Mitch Vidler, CCO

Commercial teams in iGaming are wasting valuable resources on manual data collection. In many operator organizations, highly paid commercial analysts, CRM managers, and trading specialists spend a significant portion of their week acting as manual data entry clerks. They sign up for competitor accounts, log in daily, take screenshots of homepage banners, and manually copy promotional terms into shared spreadsheets. This process is slow, expensive, and structurally flawed. By the time this manual research is compiled and analyzed, the competitive landscape has already shifted. 
In a market defined by razor-thin margins, rising taxes, and intense player multi-homing, speed to market is a major commercial differentiator. You cannot run a competitive commercial operation using stale data. 
To protect your player base and maximize promotional efficiency, you must automate your competitive research. You need an automated intelligence layer that acts as an always-on commercial radar. 
By converting manual competitor tracking into structured, near-real-time intelligence, you save hours of engineering and analysis time. You give your commercial team the visibility needed to make fast, evidence-based decisions that protect your hold rate and grow your net gaming revenue (NGR).

## The Operational Cost of Manual Data Gathering

The traditional approach to competitive intelligence in online gaming is an operational bottleneck. Commercial teams typically assign junior analysts to monitor the promotional activity of five or ten core competitors. The analysts spend their mornings manually browsing competitor websites, capturing desktop and mobile screenshots, and documenting active offers. 
This manual research is prone to human error. Analysts frequently miss segment-specific promotions, VIP-only boosts, or mid-week updates to wagering requirements. 
Furthermore, the data collected is unstructured. It remains trapped in personal spreadsheets, siloes, or presentation decks that are rarely shared across the broader organization. The CRM team does not see the trading desk's observations, and the product team remains unaware of marketing's promotional findings. 
The biggest cost of manual tracking is the loss of analytical capacity. Jurnii’s market research shows that manual competitor monitoring consumes over 30 hours per week per commercial team. 
Instead of analyzing market dynamics, optimizing promotional spend, or designing strategic campaigns, your analysts are spending their time copy-pasting text and managing spreadsheets. This manual effort represents a massive waste of intelligence and resource. It limits your decision velocity and leaves your player base vulnerable to competitor moves.

## The Scale of the Promotion Deficit

The sheer volume and velocity of the modern iGaming market makes manual tracking structurally impossible. Regulated markets like the United Kingdom, Ontario, and Sweden are hyper-active. 
In the UK market alone, major operators launch over 400 monthly promotions and more than 5,000 monthly boosts. Homepage banners, promotional sliders, and on-site messaging change multiple times a day to match live sporting events, slot game releases, or player activity cycles. 
A manual team, no matter how large, cannot monitor this volume of activity. They can only capture a tiny fraction of the active market. 
This information gap creates a severe commercial liability. If a competitor launches a highly targeted casino campaign or a temporary odds boost on a major football match, your team will likely miss it. By the time you notice a decline in active players or a drop in First Time Deposits (FTDs) in your retrospective database reports, the campaign has ended, and the players have already moved their wallet share. Jurnii’s database shows that 70% to 80% of UK players hold 3 to 5 active accounts simultaneously. They do not wait for you to match an offer. They simply switch to the app that delivers the best value.

## The Strategic Advantage of Near-Real-Time Action

Automating your competitive intelligence gives you the velocity to act before your player base churns. When Jurnii 360 alerts your team to a competitor's aggressive campaign, you can design a structured, evidence-based response immediately. 
This speed prevents the two most common errors in promotional management: underreacting and overreacting. 
Underreacting occurs when you remain unaware of competitor promotions, allowing them to capture primary wallet share from your multi-homing players. 
Overreacting occurs when you discover a competitor's offer late, panic, and launch an aggressive copycat promotion. This reactionary approach increases your promotional generosity unnecessarily, diluting your trading margins and lowering your hold rate.

You can adjust your generosity with surgical precision, protecting your hold rate while remaining highly competitive in key verticals.
