# The Defensive Playbook: How to Respond to Competitor Promo Attacks in Real-Time

By Mitch Vidler, CCO

In the hyper-competitive iGaming market, competitor promotional campaigns are not just marketing announcements. They are direct promo attacks designed to capture player wallet share. Because players hold active accounts with multiple operators, a sudden spike in competitor generosity can immediately divert your active player base. 
If your commercial team only discovers a competitor's campaign days after it launches, you are playing a losing game of reactive defense. 
Waking up to find your registration rates have dropped, or your first-time deposits (FTDs) have shifted, means you have already lost valuable players. The delay in response represents a direct cost to your Net Gaming Revenue (NGR). 
To protect your player base and defend your margins, you must automate your competitive surveillance. You need real-time change detection that monitors the market continuously. 
By reducing your competitive response time from days to under two hours, you replace panic with structured strategy. Jurnii 360's change detection and automated alerts give you the commercial visibility needed to make calm, profitable decisions that protect your hold rate and secure your player base.

## The Anatomy of a Promo Attack

Competitor promo attacks are highly tactical, fast-moving campaigns. A rival operator might launch a temporary 100% deposit match on a Friday afternoon, a high-value odds boost on a Premier League derby, or a series of free spins with zero wagering requirements on a new slot game. 
These offers are promoted aggressively on homepage sliders, pushed through CRM channels, and distributed across affiliate networks. 
Because players are multi-homers, they are highly sensitive to these temporary spikes in value. If they see a competitor offering a Promo Richness Index score of 80/100 while your standard lobby offer sits at 50/100, they will close your application. 
They deposit their weekend budget with the competitor. 
The speed of this shift is accelerated by modern mobile browser layouts and fast deposit methods. A player can switch apps and complete a deposit in less than 60 seconds. 
If you do not have visibility of the competitor's campaign as soon as it goes live, you are blind to the drain on your player base. You cannot respond to an attack you cannot see.

## The Response Lag Deficit

Most online gaming operators suffer from a severe response lag. Without automated change detection, competitor monitoring is reliant on manual sweeps. 
A typical commercial team only notices a competitor's campaign when their own trading dashboards show a drop in active player volume or a decline in deposit frequency. 
Once a drop is identified, the investigation begins. Analysts register accounts with competitor brands, navigate their lobbies, capture screenshots of banners, and manually search for the promotion's terms and conditions. 
By the time this manual research is completed, documented, and discussed in a weekly planning meeting, three days have passed. 
This response lag is a major commercial liability. 
In the online sports betting and casino market, a three-day delay means the campaign is already over. The weekend's sporting events have concluded, and the player's budget has been spent. 
Relying on manual competitor sweeps ensures you are always three days behind the market, leaving your margins vulnerable and your acquisition campaigns inefficient.

## Dissecting the Attack: The Three-Step Response Protocol

When Jurnii 360 alerts you to a competitor promo attack, your commercial team must execute a structured, evidence-based response protocol rather than a panicked copycat campaign. 
The protocol consists of three steps:
- **Step 1: Generosity Quantification:** Check the competitor's Promo Richness Index score. Do not assume a headline value of £100 is highly generous. Analyze the wagering requirements and expiry terms. If the Promo Richness Index score is low despite the large headline number, the offer is weak. You can ignore it and maintain your current promotional spend, protecting your hold rate.
- **Step 2: Exposure Assessment:** Identify which player segments are targeted. Jurnii 360 shows whether the promotion is displayed to all users or restricted to VIPs and returning players. If the attack targets a segment where you hold a strong retention advantage, you can maintain your baseline CRM strategy.
- **Step 3: Surgical Counter-Programming:** If the competitor offer is highly generous and targets your core audience, design a targeted counter-promotion. Instead of launching a broad, margin-diluting homepage banner—which increases your liability across your entire player base—execute a targeted CRM campaign to your highest-value players. Offer a lower wagering multiplier or a customized boost that matches their play history.
This structured protocol protects your trading margins. You only pay for player retention where it is mathematically necessary, preventing a race to the bottom that destroys your GGR.

When you have real-time visibility of every competitor campaign, you are no longer surprised by market shifts. You can spot historical patterns in competitor campaign launches, anticipating their promotions around major sporting events or calendar holidays. 
This visibility allows you to design your counter-promotions in advance, aligning your CRM, trading, and marketing teams before the competitor attack occurs.
