# Jurnii Helps Multi-Brand iGaming Company Unify Design for Enhanced CX

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

Effective design is a cornerstone of customer experience, especially in the competitive iGaming industry. Jurnii partnered with a multi-brand iGaming company to develop a design team strategy that aligned with their business goals, integrated key processes, and set the stage for growth.

## The Challenge

### Fragmented Design Functions Across Brands

The client faced critical challenges in their design operations:

- Decentralised design teams working in silos, leading to inconsistent user experiences.
- Gaps in expertise, particularly in areas like analytics, operations, and experimentation.
- Lack of a unified strategy to guide hiring, workflows, and team objectives.

To address these issues, the client needed a centralised design function with a clear strategy and well-defined roles and processes.

## The Solution

### A Centralised Design Team Strategy

Jurnii delivered a comprehensive framework to transform the client’s design function into a cohesive, scalable, and strategic asset.

### 1\. Stakeholder Interviews and Gap Analysis

We began with an in-depth review of the client’s current design operations through:

- Interviews with key stakeholders to understand pain points and opportunities.
- A gap analysis to identify missing capabilities and misaligned processes.

### 2\. Workshops to Shape the Vision

Collaborative workshops were held to:

- Define the strategic vision for the design team.
- Clarify roles and responsibilities (R&R) within the new structure.
- Outline process improvements to enhance cross-brand collaboration.

### 3\. Development of a Comprehensive Proposal

Based on these insights, we delivered a proposal that included:

- A detailed hiring plan to build a well-rounded design team.
- Processes for integrating design with research, analytics, operations, and experimentation functions.
- A strategy to align design efforts with broader business objectives.

## The Outcome

### A Unified Design Function for Scalable Success

Jurnii’s collaboration resulted in a transformed design function that empowered the client to deliver consistent, high-quality user experiences across their brands. Key achievements include:

1.  **Centralised Design Team**: A 12-member team was established, fostering collaboration and scalability across the company’s portfolio.
2.  **Expanded Capabilities**: The inclusion of specialists in research, analytics, operations, and experimentation enriched the team’s ability to deliver data-driven, user-focused designs.
3.  **Defined Strategy and Processes**: A unified team strategy and streamlined workflows improved efficiency and alignment with product roadmaps.
