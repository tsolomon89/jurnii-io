# From Audit to Interface: A Framework for Implementing 70+ UX Recommendations

By Tristan Dunk, CPO

Finding user experience flaws in an online gaming product is relatively straightforward. If you run a standard usability audit, use heatmaps, or conduct user testing session reviews, you will quickly identify dozens of friction points. The real challenge is execution. 
In the complex iGaming environment, translating diagnostic findings into live, compliant, and high-converting product improvements is an operational bottleneck. 
Many operators invest in comprehensive consulting audits only to receive a 150-page presentation slide deck. This document outline is too dense, lacks commercial priority, and fails to account for technical feasibility. 
As a result, the audit is archived, and the friction points continue to leak first-time deposits (FTDs) and reduce player lifetime value. 
At Jurnii, we believe that diagnostic audits are only valuable if they lead to rapid product execution. Jurnii Studio's Design Solutions is built to bridge this execution gap. 
By using a structured translation framework, we help product and engineering teams convert the Jurnii UX platform's 70+ ranked recommendations into live, optimized interface components, accelerating your release cycles and protecting your net gaming revenue (NGR).

## The Operational Bottleneck of Static Audits

Static consulting audits fail because they are disconnected from the reality of product engineering. When an external agency delivers a generic list of usability issues, the product manager faces three major obstacles. 
First, the recommendations lack technical prioritization. A suggestion to "redesign the entire sports betting slip" is treated with the same weight as a suggestion to "increase font contrast on the terms link." 
Second, the recommendations ignore the complexity of the operator's technical stack. Product teams cannot easily replace legacy third-party registration flows or modify sports betting engines that are locked into long-term vendor contracts. 
Third, the recommendations overlook regional regulatory compliance. Every change to a registration form, deposit limit interface, or responsible gaming dashboard must comply with strict licensing guidelines. 
Without this compliance context, engineering teams reject design changes out of fear of regulatory violations. 
Consequently, the audit document collects digital dust. The product team continues to build new features on top of a leaking foundation, wasting engineering resource and failing to move key commercial metrics.

## The Three-Step Translation Framework

Jurnii Studio uses a structured three-step framework to translate the 70+ recommendations generated by Jurnii UX into active product improvements. 
### Step 1: Categorization by Technical Feasibility and Compliance
We first divide the audit recommendations into three execution tiers:
- **Tiers 1 (Quick Wins):** Simple front-end modifications, such as CSS tweaks, copy adjustments, contrast fixes, and trust signal placement updates. These changes require minimal engineering resource and can be deployed in standard sprint cycles.
- **Tier 2 (Mid-Term Adjustments):** Flow-level changes that require modification to validation logic, error handling, or on-screen micro-interactions, such as optimizing the KYC upload screen or deposit input feedback.
- **Tier 3 (Long-Term Upgrades):** Core architectural updates, such as replacing legacy hybrid wrappers or migrating payment gateways, which require cross-team coordination and longer roadmap planning.
### Step 2: Commercial Severity Mapping
Once categorized, we map each recommendation to its Jurnii UX commercial severity score. This score quantifies the exact financial leak caused by the usability issue. 
For example, if the database shows that 15% of players abandon the registration flow at the postal code input field due to a validation error, that issue is mapped as a high-priority commercial leak. 
By combining technical feasibility with commercial impact, the product manager can build an evidence-based roadmap. You no longer prioritize features based on internal opinions or designer preferences. Instead, you focus engineering resource on the specific quick wins that will drive the highest increase in conversion.
### Step 3: Component-Level Prototyping
Jurnii Studio does not deliver abstract design advice. We provide pixel-perfect, component-level prototypes in Figma, accompanied by production-ready CSS and HTML code snippets. 
If Jurnii UX recommends optimizing the deposit confirmation button to include security padlocks and processing speeds, Jurnii Studio designs the exact button component. 
The design matches your brand's existing design system, ensuring consistency across all screens. Your front-end engineers do not need to interpret abstract design layouts. They can inspect the component, copy the code, and drop it directly into your active repository, reducing front-end development cycles by up to 40%.

## Designing for Compliance and Conversion

In the regulated online gaming market, design cannot exist without compliance. Every user interface element must satisfy regional regulations regarding age verification, responsible gaming limits, and player safety warnings. 
Jurnii Studio's designers specialize in iGaming compliance. When we design components for registration, deposits, or identity verification, we integrate regulatory requirements into the layout from the beginning. 
For example, we ensure that deposit limit screens present mandatory responsible gaming choices clearly, without hiding them or using misleading patterns. We place safety messages in high-visibility areas while maintaining a clean layout that keeps the player focused on the task. 
By designing compliant-by-default interface components, we eliminate the need for lengthy reviews by your internal compliance and legal teams. The design is approved faster, allowing you to deploy conversion-driving optimizations without risking regulatory pushback.

## Collaborative Execution: The Co-Development Model

Jurnii Studio does not operate as an isolated agency that delivers designs and walks away. We work as a collaborative execution partner. 
Our designers and front-end specialists integrate directly with your product managers, UX researchers, and engineering teams. We participate in sprint planning, share Figma workspace files, and support the QA process during implementation. 
This co-development model ensures that design solutions are optimized for your active technical constraints. If your engineers identify a backend API limitation that prevents a proposed interaction, we redesign the component in real-time. 
This collaborative agility prevents design stagnation. It ensures that the recommendations identified by the Jurnii UX platform are actually built, tested, and shipped to production, giving your brand the experience advantage needed to outperform the market.
