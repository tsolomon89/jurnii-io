# Optimising the UX of a Social Casino

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

See how Jurnii enhanced the user experience of a leading social casino — improving usability, engagement, and retention through data-driven UX insights and design.

## Approach: Heuristic Benchmarking

Sweeptastic, a leading social casino platform, engaged Jurnii Studio to enhance the usability and effectiveness of its customer journeys. The goal was to assess the platform’s performance against competitors and identify key areas for improvement.

Our methodology centered on **heuristic competitor benchmarking**, a process where UX experts evaluated Sweeptastic’s interface against industry best practices and direct competitors. The assessment focused on critical customer journeys, such as:

- **Depositing & Purchasing**
- **Playing Games**
- **Design & Performance**

Using a proprietary heuristic scorecard, we systematically identified friction points, usability gaps, and opportunities for design optimization.

## Executive Summary: Key Findings & Competitive Insights

Sweeptastic’s **Journey Effectiveness Score** was **65%**, slightly below the **71% industry average**. However, the platform excelled in:

✅ **Finding Games** (74% vs. 70% industry avg.)

✅ **Discounts & Promotions** (88% vs. 83% industry avg.)

While Sweeptastic outperformed competitors in these areas, it faced **usability challenges** in:

🔻 **Depositing & Purchasing** (61% vs. 78% avg.)

🔻 **Playing Games** (63% vs. 86% avg.)

🔻 **Overall Design & Performance** (57% vs. 88% avg.)

## Design Improvements: From Insights to Execution

Based on our analysis, Jurnii Studio collaborated with Sweeptastic’s team to implement targeted UX enhancements, including:

### 🔹 **Refined Game Discovery Experience**

- Improved **game categorisation** and **recommendation algorithms**
- Enhanced **key action urgency** and **casino lobby hierarchy** for easier navigation

### 🔹 **Optimized Payment & Deposit Flows**

- Simplified **checkout process** to reduce friction
- Clearer **transaction feedback** to improve user confidence

### 🔹 **Revamped UI for Mobile Performance**

- Addressed **layout inconsistencies** for better mobile usability
- Increased **CTA visibility** for key actions like purchasing and game entry
