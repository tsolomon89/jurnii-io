# Driving Platform Growth & Data-Driven Insights for Heuston Gaming

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

Heuston Gaming Limited is a rapidly growing digital gaming platform that had recently launched its proprietary product to customers. With a strong focus on user acquisition and retention, the business needed to better understand its customer base, optimise the platform experience, and build a scalable foundation for future growth.

## The Challenge

Upon launch, Heuston Gaming faced a common challenge: **limited visibility into user behaviour and performance metrics**. At the same time, their internal technology team was undergoing a major migration, leaving a gap in the management of critical tracking infrastructure.

This created two urgent needs:

1.  Establish a robust analytics framework to capture actionable data.
2.  Maintain continuity in GTM (Google Tag Manager) operations, affiliate tracking, and partner integrations during a period of internal transition.

## Key Objectives

Jurnii was engaged to:

- Define a set of actionable improvement recommendations for the platform experience.
- Build an insight and reporting framework, including the deployment of a product analytics layer.
- Ensure continuity and smooth execution of GTM and technical partner support during the internal team migration.

## Our Approach

### 1\. GTM Tag Management & Implementation

Jurnii provided full technical ownership of GTM operations, ensuring partner and marketing tags (e.g., affiliate channels, Snapchat campaigns) were configured and functioning correctly.

- Implemented new tags and retired obsolete ones.
- Conducted debugging and compliance checks with partner specifications.
- Provided detailed monthly reporting on all completed and pending tasks.

### 2\. Partner & Affiliate Technical Support

Acted as the primary technical point of contact for all affiliate and partner integrations:

- Validated tag functionality per partner requirements.
- Documented partner-specific implementation procedures.
- Maintained operational continuity for marketing campaigns.

### 3\. Data Layer Setup & Analytics Reporting

To provide Heuston Gaming with actionable insight into user behavior, Jurnii:

- Conducted stakeholder interviews to define data layer requirements aligned with business goals.
- Built a robust tracking layer to capture key user interactions and platform events.
- Integrated a product analytics tool and set up initial dashboards for brand and behavioural reporting.

## Deliverables

- Fully operational **data tracking layer** with product analytics integration.
- **Partner-compliant GTM tags** and ongoing debugging support.
- **Process execution reports** ensuring alignment with existing audit frameworks.
- Initial **analytics dashboards** delivering real-time insights into user engagement and conversion.

## Client Feedback: Impact & Results

### Results

- Established a scalable **data infrastructure** to support long-term growth.
- Enabled real-time **performance monitoring** for marketing and affiliate channels.
- Reduced the risk of data loss or campaign disruption during internal transitions.
- Delivered a clearer understanding of **user behaviours**, allowing the Heuston Gaming team to prioritize platform improvements.
