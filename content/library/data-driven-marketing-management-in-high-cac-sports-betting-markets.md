### 1. Econometric Attribution over Flawed Last-Click BI

Marketing teams frequently over-invest in branded search ads and affiliate coupon sites because last-click reporting models falsely credit those channels with conversions driven by upper-funnel brand media. Econometric MMM reveals the true incremental contribution of every channel, ensuring media spend is allocated where it drives genuine net growth.

### 2. Real-Time Competitor Proposition Intelligence

A marketing manager cannot optimise creative messaging without knowing what rivals are offering simultaneously. When Jurnii 360 detects that a competitor has launched an aggressive odds boost on a Saturday football derby, the marketing team can immediately adjust their own ad copy to highlight superior payout speeds or alternative accumulator concessions.

### 3. Funnel Experience Alignment

Driving high-intent traffic to a sluggish, 14-field registration form destroys marketing ROI. Marketing managers must collaborate directly with product teams, using [Jurnii UX](/products/jurnii-ux) to benchmark onboarding completion rates and eliminate conversion bottlenecks.

### 4. Margin-Aware Promotional Architecture

Using the **Promo Richness Index**, marketing teams evaluate the mathematical cost of acquisition bonuses before launch, avoiding loss-leading campaigns that attract non-converting bonus hunters.
