# Bitcoin and the Bible

It's just a myth that consensus requires centralized authority.

## Distributed Consensus

Both decentralized monetary networks and historical scriptural transmission rely on distributed validation protocols rather than top-down decrees.

$$\text{Consensus}(N) = \prod_{i=1}^N V_i(x)$$

When information is verified across independent nodes, the integrity of the record is preserved over long temporal horizons.
