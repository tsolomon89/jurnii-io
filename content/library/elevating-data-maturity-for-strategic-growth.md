# Elevating Data Maturity for Strategic Growth

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

Jurnii Studio guided a US-based enterprise in transforming its data strategy through a Data Maturity Assessment, strategic recommendations, and a phased roadmap. By aligning data practices with business goals, the client gained clear governance, actionable insights, and a path to unlock data as a true strategic asset.

## Client Overview

With a portfolio spanning multiple brands and products, the client faced challenges in maintaining consistency and efficiency across their digital experiences:

A leading enterprise operating out of the US was looking to optimise its data strategy. Faced with challenges such as siloed data practices, inconsistent analytics capabilities & tooling, and a lack of alignment between business goals and data initiatives, the organisation sought a roadmap to harness data as a strategic asset.

## Objective

Jurnii’s Comprehensive Design System Development

Jurnii conducted a comprehensive Data Maturity Assessment to evaluate the current state of the organisation's data capabilities, identify gaps, and outline actionable strategies to align data practices with business objectives.

## Approach

### 1\. Data Maturity Assessment

The project commenced with a comprehensive evaluation of the client's data practices using Jurnii's proprietary **Data Maturity Framework**.

The assessment focused on six core areas essential for building a robust data strategy.

1.  **Asset**: Enhancing data collection, accuracy, accessibility, and utility to maximise the organisation's data as a valuable asset.
2.  **Experimentation**: Developing processes to measure the impact of business changes and foster continuous improvement.
3.  **Measurement**: Establishing a cohesive set of targeted, accountable, and correlated metrics.
4.  **Effectiveness**: Optimizing decision-making processes, operational efficiency, and ensuring a positive ROI.
5.  **Intelligence**: Strengthening capabilities to understand and predict both customer behaviour and business outcomes.
6.  **Platform**: Evolving the data infrastructure to meet current and future operational and analytical needs.

The assessment incorporated stakeholder interviews, surveys, and benchmarking to provide a detailed view of the organisation’s maturity level in these areas.

### 2\. Strategic Recommendations

Based on the insights gained from the assessment, and through a series of stakeholder workshops, we identified a tailored set of strategic objectives within each focus area:

Some of the examples were:

- Development of an optimised processes for data collection, wrangling, analysis and governance
- Expansion of Experimentation capabilities
- Integration of a North Star Metric and KPI Tree
- Development of an advanced forecasting model and budgeting process

### 3\. Initiative Delivery Roadmap and Governance

To ensure sustainable and effective implementation, Jurnii proposed the creation of a **Data Strategy Board**, tasked with overseeing the roadmap delivery across functions. This roadmap was prioritised by:

- **Phase 1: Quick Wins**
    -   Address immediate gaps in data accuracy and accessibility.
    -   Launch metrics standardisation initiatives.
- **Phase 2: Capability Building**
    -   Implement tools and processes for experimentation and analytics.
    -   Upskill employees in data literacy and predictive analytics.
- **Phase 3: Strategic Evolution**
    -   Fully operationalise the evolved data platform.
    -   Embed advanced intelligence capabilities into core business workflows.

The newly formed **Data Strategy Board** served as the central governance body, facilitating cross-functional collaboration and driving accountability. This board included representatives from key business units to ensure comprehensive oversight and the successful execution of each initiative.

### 4\. Executive Presentation

The culmination of the project was a strategic presentation to the executive leadership team. Jurnii provided:

- An overview of current data maturity findings.
- A clear articulation of business benefits tied to the proposed initiatives.
- A high-level roadmap and timeline to achieve desired outcomes.

The presentation empowered the leadership team to make informed decisions and secure buy-in from key stakeholders.
