# Understanding AI agents for CX, UX, and product leaders

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

The term 'AI Agent' is everywhere. But for CX, UX, and product leaders, what does it actually mean for your strategy? This isn't about adopting the latest trend, but about fundamentally reshaping customer experience through AI. In this article, we provide clarity on when and how to implement AI Agents into your existing CX strategy and outline the balancing act when delivering value through AI-enabled solutions versus human-AI collaboration.

## What are AI agents, actually?

[**Anthropic**](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) differentiates between AI workflows and true AI Agents.

- **AI Workflows:** Essentially intelligent scripts for CX processes – predetermined, efficient, and lacking independent decision-making. They excel at guiding users or automating routine tasks.
- **True AI Agents:** Dynamic. They autonomously direct processes and tools, adapt to feedback, and choose their own paths. This goes beyond following instructions; it's about actively assessing and responding to the customer's journey, similar to how a human would navigate this complex process.

## Harnessing true agent systems

True AI Agents redefine adaptability. They don't just follow patterns; they analyse complex inputs. This means they can independently utilise tools—like APIs or data platforms—and, critically, they adapt based on contexual feedback.

For CX, this translates to systems that can learn from evolving customer behaviours, proactively identify emerging trends, and even self-optimise parts of the customer journey. This is not merely automation; it's about constructing an intelligent, responsive CX ecosystem.
