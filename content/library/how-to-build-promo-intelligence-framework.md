# How to build a competitor promotion intelligence framework

By Fraser Dunk

## The Short Version

- Most operators have promo intelligence as a process, not a system.
- Seven steps, in order: scope, schema, capture cadence, change detection, scoring, surfacing, review.
- Each step has a failure mode. We've hit all of them.

## Step 1 — Define the competitor set

Not "the top 10 operators." Define them by what you actually compete for. Same player segment, same vertical, same regulatory regime. Five to ten brands, named, agreed.

Failure mode: a "top 20" list nobody actually believes, that gets quietly de-scoped six months in.

## Step 2 — Define the schema

Every offer captured needs the same structured fields: type, headline value, mechanic, wagering, qualifying odds, eligibility window, channel observed, segment.

Failure mode: free-text capture. You will not be able to compare anything in six months.

## Step 3 — Set the cadence

Daily, full-coverage. Anything less and you miss launches by definition.

Failure mode: weekly. Acceptable until the week a competitor launches a one-week-only £100 free bet for a major event and you find out on Twitter.

## Step 4 — Build change detection

Not "did the page change" — "did the structured field change." Diff at the schema level.

Failure mode: page-diff alerting. You'll get 200 alerts a day and read none of them by week three.

## Step 5 — Score every offer

A composite richness index that normalises across mechanics. So a £50 free bet and a £20 bonus + £10 free spins are comparable on a single number.

Failure mode: "headline value" only. You're a pricing analyst now, congratulations.

## Step 6 — Surface to the right people

Trading sees price boosts. CRM sees reload mechanics. Product sees site-section placement changes.

Failure mode: a single weekly digest emailed to a distribution list. Nobody reads it.

## Step 7 — Review weekly, recalibrate quarterly

Weekly: did we react to anything? Quarterly: are we capturing the right brands, the right markets, the right schema?

Failure mode: never reviewing. The system rots quietly.
