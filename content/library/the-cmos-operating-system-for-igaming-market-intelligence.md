The role of the Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) in digital gaming has undergone a dramatic transformation. A decade ago, gaming CMOs focused primarily on creative brand advertising, celebrity endorsements, sponsorship negotiations, and managing digital performance agencies.

In modern regulated iGaming, the CMO operates as a core financial architect. Marketing spend represents the largest discretionary budget in the enterprise, often consuming 25% to 40% of Gross Gaming Revenue. Concurrently, marketing leadership faces unprecedented headwinds: escalating Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC), privacy tracking restrictions, tightening advertising regulations, and intense multi-homing competition where 75% of players hold accounts with competing brands.

Operating in this volatile environment with fragmented data is no longer viable. When marketing leaders rely on isolated agency dashboards, siloed last-click BI reports, and subjective competitor screenshots, they cannot defend their media investments to the board or optimise capital allocation.

To lead with commercial authority, marketing executives require a unified [CMO operating system](/use-cases/cmo). By integrating competitive intelligence in [Jurnii 360](/products/jurnii-360), econometric attribution in [Jurnii Cortex](/products/jurnii-mmm), and experience benchmarking in [Jurnii UX](/products/jurnii-ux), modern CMOs transform marketing into a predictable engine of commercial growth.

### 1. Competitive Proposition Radar

A CMO cannot optimise acquisition campaigns without knowing what competitors are offering in the same search results and social feeds. Continuous telemetry tracks rival promotional generosity and wagering restrictions, ensuring your brand never enters an acquisition window at an uncalculated proposition disadvantage.

### 2. Econometric Marketing Mix Modelling

By replacing vulnerable user-level tracking with econometric MMM, the CMO measures the true incremental contribution of every channel: from pitch-side stadium LED boards to paid search keywords. This enables evidence-based budget allocation that eliminates wasted spend on saturated media.

### 3. Funnel Experience Optimization

Marketing does not end when a prospective bettor clicks an ad; it ends when they fund their account. The CMO OS audits onboarding and cashier journeys continuously, ensuring media spend is not wasted on high-friction registration funnels.

### 4. Board-Level Commercial Alignment

The CMO OS translates complex marketing metrics (impressions, CTR, CPA) into the language of the CFO and board: Net Gaming Revenue lift, marginal ROI, and customer lifetime value (LTV).
