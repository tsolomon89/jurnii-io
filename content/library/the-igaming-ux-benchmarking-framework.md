# The iGaming UX Benchmarking Framework

A systematic breakdown of the 60+ criteria required to score player journeys from registration to first deposit.

## Quantitative Friction Scoring

$$\text{FrictionScore} = \sum_{j=1}^K w_j \cdot \tau_j$$

Where $\tau_j$ is journey step latency and $w_j$ is step drop-off weight.
