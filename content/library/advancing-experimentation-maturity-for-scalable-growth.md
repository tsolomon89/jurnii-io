# Advancing Experimentation Maturity for Scalable Growth

By Fraser Dunk, Founder & CEO

In today’s fast-moving digital landscape, data-driven experimentation is the key to continuous innovation and business optimization. Jurnii Studio partnered with a leading organization to enhance their experimentation maturity, embedding a structured server-side testing framework and fostering a culture of evidence-based decision-making.

## The Challenge

With a portfolio spanning multiple brands and products, the client faced challenges in maintaining consistency and efficiency across their digital experiences:

## Scaling Experimentation for Impact

The client faced several key hurdles in their experimentation efforts:

- **Limited capabilities**, relying primarily on front-end testing.
- **No standardized metrics** to assess experiment success.
- **Siloed experimentation efforts**, making it difficult to integrate within **development workflows**.

To fully leverage experimentation for **growth and innovation**, they needed a **scalable framework** that aligned testing with **business objectives**.

## The Solution

Jurnii’s Comprehensive Design System Development

## A Comprehensive Experimentation Framework

Jurnii Studio implemented a **three-phase strategy** to systematically build, embed, and scale experimentation across the organization.

### 1\. Experimentation Maturity Assessment

We began with a **diagnostic review** of their current capabilities, benchmarking their **maturity level** against industry standards to identify **gaps and opportunities**.

### 2\. The Three-Phase Growth Model: Start > Build > Scale

Our **structured approach** ensured the client progressed **seamlessly** through each stage of experimentation maturity:

🔹 **Start** → Established foundational practices, including process design and **early experiment planning**.

🔹 **Build** → Integrated experimentation workflows into development cycles with **server-side capabilities**.

🔹 **Scale** → Drove organization-wide adoption by implementing **advanced tools, shared learnings, and team collaboration frameworks**.

### 3\. Establishing a Centre of Excellence (CoE)

To ensure long-term sustainability, we built a **Centre of Excellence (CoE)** focused on:

✅ **Defining standardized processes & tools**

✅ **Delivering training programs** to upskill teams

✅ **Ensuring cross-team alignment** on best practices

### 4\. Assigning Measurement Champions

We introduced **Measurement Champions** within different business functions to:

- **Oversee experimentation roadmaps**
- **Track experiment performance** and ensure actionable insights
- **Facilitate data-driven decision-making across teams**

## The Outcome

A Unified Design System for Scalable Success

## Embedding Experimentation into Development DNA

Our collaboration delivered **tangible improvements**, including:

✅ **Transition to Server-Side Experimentation**: Shifting from front-end to **server-side testing** allowed for deeper insights and more reliable results.

✅ **Standardized Experimentation Reporting**: A **consistent framework** for tracking and analyzing results improved decision-making accuracy.

✅ **Creation of a Metric Repository**: Centralizing key product and business metrics enhanced **visibility** and ensured alignment across teams.

✅ **Widespread Experimentation Adoption**: Within **six months**, **30% of all new feature releases** were conducted via an experiment—up from **0%**.
