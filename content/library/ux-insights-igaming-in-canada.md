# UX Insights: iGaming in Canada

By Tristan Dexter, Chief Experience Officer

We recently ran a UX performance report across five leading iGaming brands that operate in Canada: BC.Game, Stake, Shuffle, Betty, and PlayOJO. The data highlights a competitive Canadian iGaming market, with strong UX foundations in place, but clear gaps in customer experience (CX) where user trust and engagement start to break down.

Betty emerged as the strongest overall performer, driven by a high brand perception score from users. BC Game (78) and Stake (74) performed particularly well in user journey effectiveness, supported by clear feature sets and intuitive navigation. However, technical UX performance  (including site speed, accessibility, and responsiveness) scored lower across all five brands, pointing to an industry-wide challenge.

## Key UX findings from Canadian iGaming platforms

Several consistent UX insights appeared across the platforms

- Most brands that operate in Canada have clear layouts, well-structured homepages and consistent icons.

- Scores drop when users need help: support journeys and service responsiveness are common weak points.

- Messaging and visual hierarchy vary wildly between platforms, with some elements lacking urgency or clarity.

- Brand perception is mixed across the board

- Users often feel confused by conditions and unsupported during withdrawals.

## What this means for iGaming UX and CX performance

Strong UX design scores suggest that many brands have the fundamentals right. However, customer experience suffers at high-impact moments, particularly when users need help or reassurance.

Journey friction reduces trust. Poor communication, weak feedback loops, and visually repetitive layouts contribute to hesitation and drop-off. In a market where iGaming features are increasingly similar, support experience, withdrawal transparency, and UX clarity become key differentiators.

## UX recommendations for iGaming brands

Based on the data, we identified several UX improvements that could significantly improve overall Jurnii UX scores and customer satisfaction:

- Ensure trial modes are present so users are learning game mechanics risk-free, therefore increasing user confidence.

- Be clearer with rules. Dense text explaining terms reduces clarity and motivation.

- Reduce visual fatigue. Monotonous dark card grids make it hard to scan or find patterns.

- Add urgency where it matters. CTAs are overloaded with text, reducing clarity.

- Ensure trust by including fee transparency during depositing process.

As competition in the Canadian iGaming sector intensifies, the brands that stand out will be those that invest not just in features, but in clear UX design, transparent journeys, and trustworthy customer experience, especially at moments that matter most.
