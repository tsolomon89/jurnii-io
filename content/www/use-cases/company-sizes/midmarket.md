## The Pressure Under Audit

Scaling operators face high operational friction when entering new regulated markets (e.g., Ontario, Sweden, UK). At this scale, teams become siloed; product managers ship features blindly without competitive context, while CRM teams copy rival promotions reactively, risking severe margin dilution.
