# Jurnii UX

Replace UX opinion with structured benchmarking. Jurnii UX scores every step of every critical journey across your operator and your peer set.

## Platform Metrics

- **300+** — Global Brands Scanned
- **70+** — Recommendations per Audit
- **4** — Core UX Dimensions Analysed
- **Mins** — Time to Full Automated Report

## The Commercial Challenge

iGaming operators waste months debating visual tweaks and relying on outdated agency heuristics while players drop off due to silent, unmeasured usability friction.

### Opinion-Based Roadmaps

Product decisions are often dominated by internal design debates rather than quantitative customer journey friction, causing critical deposit leaks to remain unresolved for months.

### Revenue-Mapped Prioritisation

Instantly scan player pathways, map conversion roadblocks, and weight every flaw by revenue consequence, helping product teams deploy high-impact conversion fixes first.

## Operational Transformation

### Subjective Design Debates

- ❌ Roadmaps are dominated by internal speculation and loudest-voice opinions.
- ❌ Technical layout flaws like shift layout latency remain unmeasured.
- ❌ UX agency audits take weeks to compile and fail to map revenue impact.
- ❌ Registration drop-offs are blamed on marketing channel quality.

### Commercially Prioritised Roadmaps

- ✅ Automated radial scorecards map usability variables in minutes.
- ✅ Direct layout shift parsing flags structural deposit leaks.
- ✅ Instant recommendations prioritised by commercial risk levels.
- ✅ Direct conversion fixes protect engineering capacity.

## Who Uses This Product

### Product Leadership

**Primary Goal**: Roadmap ROI

Product teams align design sprints with revenue targets, defending engineering capacity against arbitrary aesthetic requests.

### Conversion Optimisation

**Primary Goal**: Funnel Lift

CRO specialists identify exact funnel bottlenecks on desktop and mobile, ensuring smooth player onboarding curves.

### Engineering & QA

**Primary Goal**: Layout Integrity

Engineering teams track specific layout shifts and validation errors flagged by automated diagnostics without scanning manually.
