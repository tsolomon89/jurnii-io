## Overview

Cortex is an iGaming-native Media Mix Modelling engine built for operators. It models bonus mechanics, sporting events, regulatory changes, and competitive pressures to optimize acquisition and retention marketing budgets.

### Implementation Process

1. **Data-Readiness Assessment**: 12-point evaluation of spend granularity and KPI hygiene.
2. **Ingestion & Taxonomy**: Consolidating spend, KPI, and exogenous market data into a unified model structure.
3. **Bayesian Time-Series Modeling**: Calibrated with commercial priors for transparent ROI curves.
4. **Quarterly Optimization & Refresh**: Iterative model updates incorporating new campaigns and market shifts.
