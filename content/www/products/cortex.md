## The Commercial Challenge

Treating all campaign data under simple last-click rules creates a major risk: marketing teams over-attribute organic baseline conversions to paid channels, leading to massive wasted promotional spend.

### Disputed Spend & ROI Tension

Marketing claims high returns based on last-click attribution, but finance cannot reconcile these figures with company-level revenue reports. Decisions are stalled, and spend is wasted.

### Causal Spend Validation

Cortex models isolate natural conversion baselines and measure the true incremental lift of your promotions, giving finance and marketing a single, board-ready ledger.

### Last-Click Speculation
