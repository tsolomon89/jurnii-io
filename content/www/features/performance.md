## Executive Summary

Speed is a critical commercial buffer. Jurnii monitors technical vitals across transactional pipelines to ensure that slow assets, heavy images, and unoptimized libraries do not compromise player holds.

## The Cost of Operating Blind

High-value players are highly sensitive to delay. A 1-second lag in betslip interaction or checkout load causes immediate customer defection to regional competitors, bleeding GGR silently.

When operator stacks converge on identical platforms and third-party software structures, customer acquisition efficiency and margin retention are the only durable barriers protecting Net Gaming Revenue (NGR). Running campaigns without automated competitor alerts or continuous user-flow speed benchmarking causes permanent player defection to regional rivals.
