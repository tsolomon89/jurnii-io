## Overview

Competitor Analysis provides real-time visibility into operator promotional strategies. Instead of manually checking competitor websites or relying on ad-hoc screenshots, Jurnii captures, parses, and normalises every campaign automatically.

### Key Highlights

- **Daily Campaign Capture**: Track welcome packages, reload offers, and seasonal promotions.
- **Wagering & Terms Analysis**: Deconstruct fine print and qualification mechanics.
- **Historical Comparison**: Compare current campaign cadence against past event playbooks.
