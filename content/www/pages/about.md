# About Jurnii

Jurnii is the leading commercial intelligence and UX benchmarking platform built specifically for iGaming operators.

## Our Mission

We replace manual competitive research, subjective UX opinions, and fragmented marketing data with near-real-time automated intelligence.

### What Sets Us Apart

- **Operator-Native Domain Knowledge**: Designed specifically around bonus structures, sports calendars, and gaming player journeys.
- **Evidence-Based Insights**: Replacing guess-work with quantitative benchmark scores and verified market capture.
- **Enterprise Scale**: Trusted by Tier 1 and Tier 2 European and global operators.
