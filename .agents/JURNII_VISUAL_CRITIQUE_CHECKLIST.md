# Jurnii Visual Critique Checklist

Use this checklist during the Principal UI/UX Critique, Browser QA, and Final Creative Director Review. 
Rate each criterion on a scale of 1-10.

## Scoring Thresholds
* **Below 7:** **FAIL**. The design must be revised. It is not ready for production.
* **7–8:** **PASSABLE**. Acceptable, but should be improved if sprint scope allows.
* **9–10:** **PREMIUM**. Strong enough to propagate as a template.

## The Checklist

### 1. First Impression (Score: /10)
Does the page immediately communicate enterprise-grade intelligence? Does it look expensive and board-ready?

### 2. Brand Distinctiveness (Score: /10)
Is the design unmistakably Jurnii? Does it adhere to the dark strategy/light data rules, avoiding generic SaaS tropes?

### 3. Visual Hierarchy (Score: /10)
Is it obvious where the user should look first, second, and third? Are primary commercial arguments anchored effectively?

### 4. Section Rhythm (Score: /10)
Does the page have a dynamic, engaging flow? Does it avoid the monotonous "text block, then card grid" pattern?

### 5. CTA Clarity (Score: /10)
Are calls to action unmissable, appropriately placed in the user journey, and styled with high-contrast Brand Green?

### 6. Card Quality (Score: /10)
Are cards structured like intelligence panels rather than generic boxes? Is the typography crisp and data-forward?

### 7. Typography (Score: /10)
Is Geist used correctly? Is Geist Mono deployed effectively for data? Are headings distinct from body copy?

### 8. Colour Restraint (Score: /10)
Is Brand Green used strategically? Is the color palette limited (max 4 colors in charts)? Are dark backgrounds used to create authority?

### 9. Premium Feel (Score: /10)
Does the overall fit and finish feel high-end? Are there any cheap aesthetic choices (e.g., poor drop shadows, blob shapes)?

### 10. Data/Intelligence Feel (Score: /10)
Does the visual language feel rooted in data and analytics rather than marketing fluff?

### 11. Mobile Quality (Score: /10)
Does the layout stack elegantly on small screens? Are touch targets adequate? Is the mobile experience just as premium as desktop?

### 12. Commercial Clarity (Score: /10)
Does every visual choice support the underlying business logic (NGR, churn, conversion)? Are there any purely decorative elements?

### 13. Template Reusability (Score: /10)
(For Architect/Frontend roles): Are the CSS classes semantic, reusable, and free of inline styles?
