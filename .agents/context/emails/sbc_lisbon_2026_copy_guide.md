This email positions Jurnii not as an agency or generic analytics tool, but as the **essential intelligence layer for iGaming**. Fraser Dunk’s appearance on the Innovate Stage establishes high founder authority and provides an unmissable hook for pre-booking high-value 20-minute meetings at FIL & MEO Arena.
