# KTO — Brazil Retention & Margin Context (CMO, Q2 2026)

**Thesis:** The regulated Brazilian market entered its post-acquisition phase faster than predicted. **Retention is the 2026 battleground.** SiGMA/Blask: *"As the impact of upfront bonuses fades, brands that keep players engaged longer will gain ground."* Players don't churn in Brazil — they silently reduce wallet share across Betano, Bet365, Betnacional, and KTO.

## Strategic risks (CMO frame)
- **Retention over acquisition:** as upfront bonuses fade, engagement duration wins; multi-homing means silent wallet-share dilution.
- **CazéTV World Cup window:** most concentrated acquisition event in Brazilian gambling history; every competitor prepared (Betnacional primary CazéTV WC slot, Betano FIFA Club World Cup, Bet365 Paulistão + CazéTV).
- **Margin pressure:** rising GGR tax + CIDE deposit tax make promotional efficiency non-negotiable; every point of GGR promo spend must be justified.
- **Localisation advantage is time-limited:** KTO's hyper-local position (Portuguese support, Pix-first payments, Brazilian football partnerships) is a real differentiator today, but global operators are closing the gap.
- **UX friction at scale:** CazéTV World Cup drives millions of new visitors; a registration/deposit friction point has compounding cost.
