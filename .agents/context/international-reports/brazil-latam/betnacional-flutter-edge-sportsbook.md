# BetNacional / Flutter Edge — Brazil Product Context (Q2 2026)

**Thesis:** Deploying the **Flutter Edge** sportsbook technology (built for UK/US) into Brazil is the most consequential product question in the Flutter Brazil portfolio. Global tech must land against Betano's ~27% share, Bet365's scale, and a Brazilian player who behaves differently from any other market.

## Strategic risks (Head of Product frame)
- **Flutter Edge thesis needs Brazilian product proof, not US/UK case studies** — $350M+ anchored to belief that global tech creates BR product superiority; unmeasured without surface-by-surface benchmarking vs Betano, Bet365, Novibet.
- **Betano holds ~27% and iterates product weekly** — closing a 22pp gap requires UX that outperforms the leader on registration completion, first bet, in-play UX, cashout speed.
- **CazéTV awareness expires the moment a player tries a competitor first** — 5.6M concurrent is awareness, not retention; journey execution decides conversion.
- **Public-company scrutiny** — every quarter without credible share trajectory raises cost of capital on the Brazil thesis.
