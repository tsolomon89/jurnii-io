# KTO — Brazil Product/UX Intelligence (June 2026)

**Core thesis:** KTO's sportsbook is powered by **Kambi** (B2B, signed Sep 2024) — the same core several Brazilian competitors share. When odds engine, live streaming, and core betting product are common, structural differentiation must come from **UX execution** (interface, journey flow, friction reduction). KTO's Reclame Aqui CX nomination signals internal CX focus but lacks benchmarked external proof.

## Key competitive challenges (product context)
- **Shared B2B infrastructure (Kambi):** UX is the structural differentiator, not the engine. Multiple BR competitors share the same core.
- **Roadmap on internal opinion is slower/less commercially aligned:** "vendor roadmap paralysis" — without continuous external benchmarking, teams fix what *feels* broken vs what data shows is breaking relative to competitors.
- **Post-Jan 2025 KYC friction:** Brazil's regulated KYC introduces friction at the highest-intent moment (first deposit). Competitive question is whether KTO's onboarding handles it better than Betano, Novibet, EstrelaBet, Bet365 — the operator with the smoother flow wins the first-deposit moment.

## Risk of inaction (as framed)
- Competitors can replicate engine-level decisions but not UX-level ones; the product layer on top of Kambi is the only structural moat.
- Novibet, Esportes da Sorte, EstrelaBet are setting the UX quality benchmark in the licensed BR set; without knowing where their journeys outperform KTO, roadmap prioritisation is best-guess.
- KYC onboarding friction is shared across the BR set; smoother flow captures the deposit.

## Methodology signals
- 400+ Jurnii UX data points per brand; <5% variance vs human-led audits; delivered in minutes.
- Jurnii 360 promo/boost tracking up to 4×/day; real-time competitor alerts; journey mapping side-by-side.
