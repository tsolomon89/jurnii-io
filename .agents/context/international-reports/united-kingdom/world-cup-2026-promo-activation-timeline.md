## Early Mover breakdowns
### Spreadex — "£100 in Free Bets when you Bet £10" (New customers, GB/IE)
Rolling outright reward: £10 free bet per nation win, up to 7 wins. Place £10 fixed-odds Outright Winner bet → immediately get £30 (£10 fixed odds + £10 Total Goals spread + £10 Supremacy spread); then £10 free bet per match the nation wins.
- Scenarios: 3 group wins → £60 · final (6 wins) → £90 · tournament win (max) → £100.
- **Why early mover:** first operator with a mechanic whose *value is contingent on tournament results* (sustained engagement after every match), plus spread-bet free bets unavailable to fixed-odds-only competitors.

### Betfair — "Golden Boot WC 2026" (Existing customers, opt-in)
Bet £10+ on Golden Boot market → £1 free bet builder per goal your selected player scores (credited within 24h; penalty shootout goals excluded).
- **Why early mover:** only pre-tournament offer where reward is tied directly to live match events (goal-by-goal micro-credits); targets *existing* customers pre-tournament (unusual — most operators in acquisition mode); tournament-native mechanic, not a repurposed weekly offer.
