## Portfolio themes (recurring across the estate)
- **[HIGH] Fragmented design system & legacy pages** (Bingo, Casino, Sky Bet) — new/legacy screens side by side; reset-password and help journeys feel disconnected.
- **[HIGH] Search, filtering & discoverability gaps** (all three) — no header search/filters/favourites; weakest touchpoints in the matrix.
- **[HIGH] Bonus & promotion transparency** (Bingo, Casino) — wagering, expiry, opt-in/out missing/buried; lowest touchpoint on Bingo (63) and Casino (67). Sky Bet has started addressing this with a promotion-progress indicator.
- **[HIGH] Core Web Vitals performance debt** (all three) — LCP 4–6s, TTI 14–22s from render-blocking CSS/JS and unoptimised images.
- **[MED] Outdated help content — Tombola Bingo** — help articles reference outdated UI screenshots.
- **[MED] Support responsiveness & channel gaps** (Bingo, Casino) — undisclosed response times, limited contact options, dated chat; help 65–70.
- **[MED] Form guidance & error recovery** (all three) — no placeholders/masking; errors say what's wrong but not how to fix; registration 71–79.

## Momentum — resolved this period (13, almost all Tombola Bingo)
Standardised form inputs (registration/login/deposit); "Forgot email?" recovery on login; improved password-strength indicator; deposit retry & alternative payment on failure; deposit-limit setting in deposit flow; bonus expiry dates surfaced; support response times per channel; Casino "How to play" game info made visible.
