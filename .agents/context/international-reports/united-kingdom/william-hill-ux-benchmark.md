# William Hill — Competitive UX Benchmark (UK)

## Abstract
Jurnii benchmarked the William Hill experience against four direct competitors — Paddy Power, Bet365, Sky Bet, and Ladbrokes — across eleven journey touch points. The overall journey score is 67 — two points above the market average of 65, level with Bet365. This report revisits Jurnii's September 2025 assessment: a baseline to track progress against.

William Hill's journey score sits at 67 — mid-pack, with signing-in a standout and games and support the weak links.

## 02 · Recommendations — Where to focus first
155 findings across four lenses:
- **Journeys**: 60 (18 high · 33 med · 9 low)
- **Usability**: 24 (2 high · 20 med · 2 low)
- **Performance**: 65 (23 high · 34 med · 8 low)
- **Perception**: 6 (4 high · 2 med · 0 low)

### Priority moves (14 of 47 high-severity findings)
- **[Perception · HIGH] Speed up and clarify payouts** — Slow, opaque withdrawal processing drives distrust and negative reviews. Paddy Power's fast, transparent payouts are the benchmark.
- **[Perception · HIGH] Rebuild trust in account management** — Opaque account practices and suspected unfair blocking fuel distrust. Bet365's clearer processes reduce conflict.
- **[Perception · HIGH] Fix reliability: crashes and delays** — Frequent app crashes and transaction delays hurt experience and sentiment.
- **[Perception · HIGH] Modernize customer service** — Support reads as scripted/unresponsive vs Sky Bet and Paddy Power. Move to proactive, empathetic model.
- **[Usability · HIGH] Show a success state on completed forms** — No green success indicator confirms a correctly filled form.
- **[Journeys · HIGH] Add onboarding & value proposition** — First-time users get no trust, feature, or safety context.
- **[Journeys · HIGH] Give the sign-in page a clear title** — No visible "Sign in" heading; users lack orientation.
- **[Journeys · HIGH] Surface withdrawal minimums inline** — Minimum withdrawal rules buried below the button; show dynamically by payment method before an error occurs.
- **[Journeys · HIGH] Add homepage search** — No search box/icon on homepage; add prominently in header, as Bet365 does.
- **[Performance · HIGH] Fix core accessibility failures** — Missing ARIA roles/labels, no video captions, broken keyboard focus, sub-48px tap targets recur site-wide. Target ≥95.
- **[Performance · HIGH] Cut Time to Interactive under 5s** — TTI as high as 27.4s on desktop from render-blocking and legacy JS.

## 03 · What's changed since September
Changes seen by brand: William Hill, Bet365, Ladbrokes, Sky Bet, Paddy Power. William Hill shipped changes to Registration, Getting help, and Finding games.

### Registration (73 → 87, +14)
The registration flow evolved from a typical 3-step multiple-field form to one field per step, giving each request clearer instruction, inline validation and visible reasoning — building trust while lowering perceived effort. Progress indicators moved from three generic anchor points to progressive stages showing exactly where the user is. Previous journey: 7 steps; current journey: 9 steps.
