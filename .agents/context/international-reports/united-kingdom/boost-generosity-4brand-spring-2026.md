# Boost Generosity — 4-Brand + Competition Breakdown (UK, Spring 2026)

**Key finding:** Contrasting strategic models — **Ladbrokes' high-percentage/ultra-low-cost efficiency** vs **William Hill's expensive short-odds escalation**.

|---|---|---|---|
| William Hill | 16.7% | 2.78pp | 4,102 |

## Strategic interpretation
- **William Hill — high boost, high cost.** Top-right of scatter (16–20% / 2.3–3.6pp), most expensive per boost. 18 May: 851 boosts at 20% / 3.33pp (highest single-week volume). Short-odds skew → concedes far more probability per boost.
- **Ladbrokes — high boost %, ultra-low margin (standout finding).** 16–21% median matches/exceeds WH week-for-week, yet margin conceded just 0.9–1.4pp (≈bet365). Implies boosting longer-odds selections or more efficient structuring. **Efficiency ratio ~14–20× vs WH's 5–7×** (highest of any brand).
- **Paddy Power — disciplined baseline.** 11.1% / ~1.5pp almost every week; Cheltenham (17.4% / 2.5pp) the lone deliberate deviation. Tightest cluster.
- **bet365 — lowest margin conceded across every competition.** 13–14% at 1.28pp; volume leadership in football (PL, UCL) + cost discipline = efficiency benchmark.
