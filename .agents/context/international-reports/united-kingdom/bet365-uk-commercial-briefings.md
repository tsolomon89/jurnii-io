# bet365 — UK Commercial Intelligence (April 2026)

## bet365 positioning
- **Highest boost volume in the UK market: 2,554 events / 2,491 boosts (Feb–Apr 2026)** — but **lowest SoPV at 4.1%** (15.5pp behind BoyleSports 19.6%). Median boost richness **12.5%** (vs market avg 14.3%, BoyleSports 66.7%).
- **Volume-heavy, low-intensity** — the market rewards value-density, not boost frequency. The volume strategy that worked at 21% RGD is structurally inefficient at 40%.

## UK regulatory context (from Neil briefing — notable)
- **Post-January 2026 promo rules:** **mixed-product bans, 10× wagering caps, customer-targeting restrictions** — all changed in January 2026; each operator adapting differently.
- **40% RGD** reset promotional economics (every promotional pound costs ~19p more than in March).

## Ops framing
- Most ops teams monitor competitors manually (screenshots, weekly checks, Slack); the market produced 13,673 promotional events in 60 days across 21 brands — manual monitoring cannot keep pace.
