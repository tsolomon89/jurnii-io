## Where Circus leads
- **[KSA] Only operator to cap daily time limits at 6 hours** (Unibet/Holland Casino allow 8h).
- **[KYC] Hard KYC gate before first deposit** — BSN at registration; first deposit blocked until verification clears; mandatory pre-deposit limits cannot be skipped.
- **[Withdrawals] KYC status shown before withdrawal amount entry.**
- **[Retention] Only clearly-explained VIP club** — tier/benefits/progress shown.
- **[Registration] In-flow RG** — mid-form "Speel verantwoord" block before consent (only in-flow RG placement in cohort).
- **[Unhappy flows] Cleanest error & declined-deposit handling** (100).
- **[KYC] Third-party KYC disclosed** (Jumio, iDIN, iDEAL named; ~2-min review; dual camera+file upload).

## Where Circus gives ground
- **[Getting Help] No formal complaints/ADR route** (only brand without); help off persistent header; Dutch support partial. Weakest journey (40).
- **[Account History] No net-spend or lifetime-net-position view** (Bet365 shows net spend; Holland Casino shows lifetime net position).
- **[Deposits] Thinnest payment set + no mobile payments** — 3 methods (iDEAL/Wero, cards, PayPal).
- **[Casino] No demo play, thin discovery** — lowest casino coverage.
- **[Account Access] Permanent closure needs support contact** (not self-serve); no "remember me"; no biometric/SSO.
- **[Account Access] CRUKS integration not clearly communicated** (only partial signal).
- **[Registration] One long unstepped form** (~13 field groups, no SSO/bank-ID fast path).
