# BetMGM — US Commercial Intelligence (April 2026)

## Group facts (consistent across all 4 briefings)
- **MGM Resorts / Entain joint venture.** **~20% US iGaming share — BetMGM leads.** iGaming = **71% of total revenue**. **~14% OSB share.**
- **OSB monthly active users down 16% YoY (Q1 2026)**; average actives down 9% to **597,000**. **CPA inflating materially** — Kalshi pulling acquisition dollars upstream.
- **Q1 2026 net revenue $696m (+6% YoY)** — below analyst expectation (~$767m).
- **FY26 EBITDA target revised down to $300–350m** (previously $3.1–3.2bn revenue guidance).
- **BetMGM Jurnii UX score 44/100** (tied Caesars; Placing Bets 81, Depositing critical 33).
- **Exclusive iGaming content:** Wizard of Oz, Wheel of Fortune, Elvis — its most commercially important position; FanDuel is coming for it.
- **Cannot launch prediction markets** without risking retail gaming licences (structural exclusion). **Kalshi at 89% of US prediction-market volume.**
- **World Cup 2026 in home markets (US, Canada, Mexico)** — CRO **Matt Prevost** called it a specific commercial priority; without prediction markets, won via superior sportsbook UX + promo intelligence.

## Regional / role angles
- **US West (Cory Nebel):** Nevada is the omnichannel home market (MGM Resorts properties anchor); Colorado and Arizona scaling. Lower OSB density, faster-growing iGaming where legal, prediction-market entrants reshaping CPA.
- **US East (Kathryn Francis):** NJ, PA, MI, MA — mature OSB + maturing iGaming; structurally tightest competitive set; marketing efficiency separates winners from laggards.
- **Steve Winter (Conversion & Optimization):** every CPA dollar must convert at a higher rate than competitors' — onboarding, first-bet flow, live betting must be measurably better than FanDuel/DraftKings.
- **Anthony Conroy (executive interview):** same group corporate facts (Commercial Director framing).
