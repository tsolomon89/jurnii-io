## Six strategic focus areas
1. **Raise everyday usability & product design (Usability 76, lowest in field)** — underline-only fields, missing helper text/required markers, inconsistent CTA hierarchy, primary CTA fails WCAG contrast, vague errors. Sub-scores: Flexibility & efficiency 64 (Stake leads 79), Recognition 72, User control 72, Error prevention 73, Aesthetic 74 (Bet365 leads 91), Consistency 85 (BetMGM leads 95).
2. **Make bets findable in sportsbook (Journey 43, weakest, −21)** — no header search, no filtering/sorting in bet lobby, no prioritisation of high-interest events. Bet365/BetMGM/DraftKings lead.
3. **Rebuild help & support (Journey 45)** — no visible 24/7, no response-time expectations, support buried, limited contact methods, RG absent on mobile.
4. **Smooth registration & deposit (60/62)** — users forced out of betting flow to deposit (lose betslip), no preset amounts, no registration progress/back button, input not preserved.
5. **Protect & reinforce trust (Perception 77, strength to defend)** — user doubts on RNG/fairness, slow/unclear withdrawals, unexpected account closures, misleading bonus terms.
6. **Shore up technical-performance foundation (Performance 36)** — TTI 17–40s, LCP up to 26s, CLS up to 1.3 (good = 0.1); render-blocking CSS/JS, unoptimised images, weak caching. Targets: TTI <5s, LCP <2.5s, CLS <0.1, accessibility ≥90.

## Sequencing
- **Short term (next quarter):** usability/form/CTA pass; sportsbook search & filters (largest journey gap); inline deposit + preset amounts; support visibility; performance foundation.
- **Long term (3–12 months):** design system; personalisation engine; trust infrastructure (fairness audits, security, support quality); proactive context-aware chat; performance governance (budgets in CI, RUM, edge rendering).
