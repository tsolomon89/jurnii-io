# International iGaming Intelligence, Thought-Leadership Series

Brand-neutral, region-focused research reports written in the **Jurnii house voice** (per `.agents/context/brand-guide`): calm authority, commercially literate, specific claims with numbers, no hype or UX-softness, "intelligence layer" category framing. Each develops a **single argument** (Pillar 1, *Experience is the battleground*) deeply evidenced from the crawled Quill data in `.agents/context/international-reports/`.

These pieces are about **markets and regions**, not individual operators. Operators appear only as evidence of market patterns, never as the subject, target, or hero.

## Format and voice notes
- **Register:** thought-leadership / research report. Single argument developed in depth, data exhibits, forward-looking commercial implications.
- **Owned vocabulary used:** intelligence layer, always-on / near-real-time, proprietary benchmarking, Share of Promotional Voice, margin conceded.
- **Banned vocabulary avoided:** no "revolutionary / game-changing / seamless / delightful / leverage / holistic / robust", no hedging, no exclamation marks.
- **Punctuation:** em dashes removed throughout; en dashes retained only in numeric and date ranges.
- **Byline:** house voice ("Jurnii Intelligence"). For publication, market-analysis pieces map naturally to **Fraser Dunk (CEO)** territory per the brand guide; product-mechanics angles map to Tristan Dunk (CPO); promotional-intelligence angles to Mitch Vidler (CCO).
- **Tagline used for this format:** *Where UX Meets Commercial Strategy*.
