# 08 · Visual Identity Guidelines

> How Jurnii looks. This chapter covers the established design system for employees creating presentations, reports, and marketing assets. Visual consistency reinforces brand recognition and credibility with enterprise buyers.
> 

---

## Design philosophy

Jurnii’s visual identity reflects its brand personality: calm authority, commercial precision, and intelligence. The aesthetic is clean, data-forward, and confident — not loud, not decorative, not tech-startup generic.

The primary visual environment is dark (deep dark green/near-black backgrounds with brand green accent) for hero and primary brand moments. Light (off-white surfaces) for product and content contexts. The dark brand creates authority and distinctiveness; the light mode creates clarity and usability.

## Typography

### Primary typeface — Geist

Used for all headings, body text, UI labels, and navigation. A clean, modern sans-serif with commercial legibility at small sizes.

| Style | Weight | Size (print/web) | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display | 700 | 40–72px | Hero headlines, cover slides |
| H1 | 600 | 28–36px | Page titles, primary section headers |
| H2 | 600 | 22–28px | Section headers |
| H3 | 500 | 18–22px | Subsection headers |
| Body | 400 | 16–18px | Running text |
| Small | 400 | 13–14px | Captions, labels, footnotes |

### Accent typeface — Cormorant Garamond

Used sparingly for editorial moments only: hero headline italic emphasis, pull quotes, major number callouts.

**Rules:** Italic weight only. Never used for body text. Never used for UI. One instance per page or slide maximum.

### Mono typeface — Geist Mono

Used for data labels, numerical outputs, code references, technical specifications, and platform metric displays.

## Logo usage

### Approved variants

- **Full lockup:** Jurnii wordmark + spark icon (primary). Use for all external communications, slides, and documents.
- **Icon only:** Spark mark alone. Use only when space is very constrained and brand is already established in context (e.g., social profile image, favicon).

### Minimum sizes

- Full lockup: 80px wide minimum (digital), 20mm minimum (print)
- Icon only: 24px minimum (digital)

### Clear space

Minimum clear space on all sides equal to the height of the “J” in the wordmark.

### Approved backgrounds

- Dark brand `#252c1e` — use white or brand green version of logo
- Off-white `#f8f8f7` — use dark version of logo
- White `#ffffff` — use dark version of logo

### Never do this

- Do not recolour the logo outside approved variants
- Do not place the logo on the brand green `#94ff96` background (insufficient contrast)
- Do not stretch, rotate, or skew the logo
- Do not add drop shadows or effects
- Do not use the wordmark without the spark icon in external contexts

## Presentation & report templates

### Slide types

| Slide type | Background | Text colour | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark hero | `#252c1e` | White + brand green accent | Cover slides, section dividers, key statements |
| Light body | `#f8f8f7` | Dark `#2a2a27` | Content slides, data slides, comparison slides |
| Data focus | White `#ffffff` | Dark, with green data highlights | Charts, tables, metric callouts |

### Slide density rules

- Maximum 3 key points per slide
- Every content slide must have at least one data point or visual anchor
- No full paragraphs on slides — use the notes section for narrative
- One chart or visual per slide — not multiple charts competing for attention

### Hierarchy in slides

1. The headline makes the argument (not just labels the topic)
2. The visual or data proves it
3. The body text (if any) adds one clarifying detail

**Example:**

> ❌ Headline: "Competitor Promotional Activity"
> 

> ✅ Headline: "Bet365 runs 3x more daily boosts than the UK market average"
>

## Data visualisation style

### Colour rules for charts

- Jurnii’s data / client’s position: brand green `#94ff96` or deep green `#10d03a`
- Competitors / comparators: neutral grey `#807f77` or lighter variants
- Alerts / below-benchmark indicators: use muted warm tone — avoid harsh red
- Never use more than 4 colours in a single chart

### Chart preferences

- Bar charts for comparison across a set of competitors
- Line charts for trend data over time
- Tables for detailed breakdowns (use Geist Mono for numbers)
- Avoid pie charts unless the proportion story is the entire point

### The Promo Richness Index visual

The Promo Richness Index is a core Jurnii brand asset and should be visually consistent across all materials. Reference the canonical design from the Jurnii 360 sales deck. Always show Jurnii client vs competitive set. Always show the index score prominently in Geist Mono.
