## The sales narrative arc

Every commercial conversation — from a cold outreach email to a board presentation — follows this logic sequence. It is not a script. It is a structure that ensures the right argument is made in the right order.

1. **Market context** — establish the commercial conditions: commoditisation, rising CAC, player multi-homing, manual monitoring at scale. Make the problem feel structural, not situational.
2. **Problem at scale** — name the specific problem Jurnii solves. 400+ monthly promotions. Manual UX reviews taking weeks. Analytics that show what happened, not what will happen.
3. **Why manual doesn’t work** — the volume and velocity of the market makes manual monitoring structurally impossible. This is not about effort — it’s about scale.
4. **Jurnii’s mechanism** — describe specifically how Jurnii solves the problem. Not “we use AI.” What the AI does, what it outputs, how fast.
5. **Proof** — one specific data point, one client reference (where allowed), one metric.
6. **The ask** — a 20-minute demo. Not a “chat.” Not a “call to explore synergies.” A demo with a specific question to answer.

## Objection handling — full set

### “We already have analytics.”

**Why they say it:** They are conflating internal analytics (what happened to our players) with competitive intelligence (what the market is doing around us). They are different products solving different problems.

**Response:** “Analytics tells you what happened inside your product. Jurnii tells you what the market is doing around you — before it affects your numbers. They’re not the same thing. Most of our clients run both.”

**If they push:** “What does your analytics platform tell you when a competitor launches a new promotional mechanic? How long does it take you to find out? Jurnii answers that question in near-real-time, automatically.”

---

### “We benchmark manually.”

**Why they say it:** Someone on the commercial or product team does occasional sweeps. They feel this is sufficient. They haven’t fully quantified what it costs in time, or what they’re missing between sweeps.

**Response:** “Manual benchmarking doesn’t scale at 400+ monthly promotions and 5,000+ boosts across five UK operators. Either your team is spending 30+ hours a week on it, or they’re not covering it properly. Jurnii covers it completely, automatically, every day.”

**If they push:** “The real question is what happens between your manual reviews. What moves did competitors make last week that your team doesn’t know about yet?”

---

### “AI is risky — we don’t trust AI outputs.”

**Why they say it:** They’ve had experience with generic AI tools that produce inconsistent or hallucinatory outputs. They’re right to be sceptical of those tools.

**Response:** “Jurnii’s intelligence is built on proprietary structured data — not a language model generating text from the internet. The AI analyses structured criteria against a consistent dataset of 300+ brands. The output is reproducible and commercially weighted. It reduces subjectivity — it doesn’t introduce it.”

---

### “This replaces our team.”

**Why they say it:** They’re concerned that buying Jurnii implies their existing commercial or product team is inadequate. This is often a political concern, not a logical one.

**Response:** “Jurnii augments instinct with validation. 70% of product experiments fail to meet expectations — not because teams are wrong, but because they lack the market context to test the right hypothesis. Jurnii gives your team the evidence base to move faster and be right more often.”

---

### “We have this covered internally.”

**Why they say it:** Variation of the manual benchmarking objection — usually means someone on the team is responsible for competitive monitoring but the coverage is incomplete.

**Response:** “Let’s test that. Can your team tell me what Bet365, Sky Bet, and Paddy Power promoted in the last 7 days? How their bonus values compared to yours? What creative they’re running on their homepage right now? If the answer is no, there’s a gap — and that gap is Jurnii.”

---

### “We can’t justify the cost right now.”

**Why they say it:** Budget pressure is real. This objection often means the ROI case hasn’t been made compellingly enough, not that the budget genuinely doesn’t exist.

**Response:** “Let’s put a number on the status quo. 30+ hours per week of a commercial team’s time spent on manual monitoring — at salary cost, that’s significant. Then there’s the cost of the moves you miss between manual reviews. Jurnii’s pricing is calibrated against the cost of not having the intelligence.”

**If they need a specific hook:** Use the Promo Richness Index framing — “if we can show you that you’re 23% less generous than Bet365 on horse racing, and that’s costing you on that vertical, the ROI case writes itself.”

---

### “We’re mid-contract with another tool.”

**Why they say it:** Genuine procurement constraint, or a polite delay signal.

**Response:** “Understood. When does the contract come up? And — separately — is the other tool giving you market-wide competitive intelligence, or internal data? Because those aren’t the same product, and there’s often no overlap.”

---

### “We need to involve IT / procurement.”

**Why they say it:** The process is real. But it often comes after a soft yes — the question is whether the commercial champion has the conviction to push it through.

**Response:** “Of course — what does your procurement process typically need? We’ve been through UKGC-level procurement before. Happy to provide whatever security documentation, data handling agreements, or vendor assessments are required. What’s the timeline?”

## Jurnii vs manual — comparison

| Dimension | Manual monitoring | Jurnii |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scale** | Limited to what a team can manually review | 300+ brands (UX) and full market coverage (360) |
| **Speed** | Days to weeks between reviews | Near-real-time, daily updates, instant alerts |

## Messaging by stakeholder role

These are the opening paragraph frameworks for persona-specific outreach. Do not use verbatim — they are starting points for personalisation.

### CPO

**Lead:** Evidence-based prioritisation. “Benchmark your UX against 300+ competitors with commercially weighted scoring — and prioritise your roadmap by revenue impact, not team opinion.”

**Proof point:** 70+ ranked recommendations per audit, delivered in minutes

**Ask:** “Could I show you how your current registration flow compares to your top 5 competitors in a 20-minute demo?”

### CCO

**Lead:** Competitive intelligence gap. “Know what every competitor is promoting the moment it goes live — and never again be two weeks behind a market move.”

**Proof point:** 400+ monthly promotions tracked automatically across 5 UK operators

**Ask:** “Could I show you what Bet365, Sky Bet, and Paddy Power promoted last week in a 20-minute demo?”

### CMO

**Lead:** Performance variance and MMM. “Explain your performance variance with market evidence, not guesswork — and feed competitor promotional data directly into your MMM.”

**Proof point:** MMM-ready competitive data export — pre-formatted for direct model ingestion

**Ask:** “Could I show you how Jurnii’s 360 data feeds into marketing mix modelling in a 20-minute demo?”

### CEO

**Lead:** Strategic intelligence. “The operators who win won’t spend the most — they’ll see the market most clearly. Jurnii is the intelligence layer that compounds.”

**Proof point:** Data moat framing — every month of data compounds into an asset no competitor can replicate

**Ask:** “Could I walk you through how [named competitor] is positioned against your brands right now in a 20-minute demo?”
