## Headline
**The intelligence layer for iGaming**
Compete on Experience. Win on Intelligence.

## The Challenge
iGaming operators are flying blind in a commoditised market.
1. **Manual & reactive:** UX benchmarking done by hand takes weeks. By the time insight arrives, the market has already moved.
2. **Siloed intelligence:** Product, marketing, and leadership operate from different data — no shared commercial view.
3. **Rising CAC:** With 3–5 accounts per player, loyalty is fragile. Minor friction compounds and kills conversion silently.
4. **No attribution:** Operators can't measure the true impact of one of the key levers of their marketing spend — their promo and pricing offering.

## Who Jurnii Is
Not an agency. Not an analytics tool. An intelligence layer.
Jurnii is the proprietary commercial intelligence platform for iGaming operators. We automate UX benchmarking and competitor proposition tracking at scale — transforming manual research, subjective opinion, and reactive analytics into structured, near-real-time intelligence.

- **Proactive > reactive:** Identify what will go wrong before users churn.
- **Continuous intelligence:** Always-on benchmarking, not point-in-time snapshots.
- **Commercial relevance:** Connected directly to NGR, churn, and conversion.

## The Three-Product Suite
**Three products. One intelligence layer.**
Each product feeds the others — UX data enriches 360; 360 data powers Cortex MMM models.

### 1. Jurnii UX (UX Intelligence)
**Proactive UX intelligence.** AI-powered UX audit platform that analyses iGaming brands across 4 key dimensions, delivering 70+ ranked, commercially weighted recommendations in minutes — not weeks.
- **Key Metrics:** 70+ Ranked recommendations per audit, 4 Key UX dimensions analysed, Minutes Time to full audit, 300+ Brands analysed since launch
- **Capabilities (The 4 Dimensions):** 
  1. Journey effectiveness
  2. Usability
  3. Performance
  4. Perception

### 2. Jurnii 360 (Commercial Radar)
**Automated competitor proposition intelligence.** Track every promotion, bonus, and campaign across your competitive set — updated daily with real-time alerts, structured for MMM use.
- **Key Metrics:** 1,000+ Offers tracked per week, 35 Markets monitored simultaneously, 40+ hrs Saved per week vs manual monitoring, Hours Competitive response time
- **Capabilities:**
  1. Live promotion tracking
  2. Timing & sequencing
  3. Offer benchmarking
  4. Trend spotting
  5. Real-time alerts
  6. Historical database

### 3. Cortex (Marketing Attribution)
**Marketing intelligence & attribution.** Turns UX and competitor/proposition data into attribution and planning intelligence.
- **Capabilities:**
  - AI analytics assistant
  - Causal impact & MMM
  - Cross-channel Gantt
  - Finance reconciliation
  - AI snapshot reports
  - Scenario planning
