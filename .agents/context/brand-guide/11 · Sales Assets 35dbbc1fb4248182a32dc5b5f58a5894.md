# 11 · Sales Assets

> This page defines every sales asset Jurnii needs, what each one does in the sales process, what it should contain, and how it should be used. Assets are organised by stage of the sales journey — from first contact through to post-signature. Each asset proposal includes a rationale, format recommendation, and build brief.
> 

---

## How to read this page

Assets are grouped into five stages that mirror the Jurnii sales motion:

1. **Pre-demo** — assets that earn the meeting
2. **Demo** — assets used during the conversation
3. **Post-demo** — assets that convert the conversation to a pilot
4. **Retention & expansion** — assets that protect and grow existing clients
5. **Events & partners** — assets for in-person and channel contexts

Each asset entry covers: what it is, what job it does in the sales process, what it should contain, format, and the input required to build it.

> **Build priority:** Assets marked 🔴 are blocking pipeline conversion and should be built first. Assets marked 🟡 are high value but not immediately blocking. Assets marked 🟢 are important for scale but can follow.
>

## Stage 1 — Pre-demo assets

*Assets that create enough credibility and relevance to earn a 20-minute demo request.*

---

### 1.1 Outbound email templates by persona 🔴

**What it is:** A set of four persona-specific outreach frameworks — one each for CPO, CCO, CMO, and CEO — that give the commercial team a consistent, on-brand starting point for all outreach.

**Job in the sales process:** The entry point for all cold and warm outreach. Replaces improvised emails with structured, tested frameworks that open with a relevant data hook and close with a single specific ask: a 20-minute demo.

**What it should contain:**

- Hook line (specific data point or market observation relevant to their role)
- One sentence on the commercial problem Jurnii solves for that persona
- One sentence on the Jurnii mechanism
- The ask: "Could I show you [specific thing] in a 20-minute demo?"
- Total: under 80 words

**Format:** Notion page with four template blocks, each labelled by persona. Should include a worked example for each persona alongside the blank framework.

**Example — CCO version:**

> We tracked 400+ promotions across the UK market last month. Bet365 ran 3x more daily boosts than the market average — and launched 72 hours before the weekend's biggest fixtures.
> 

> 
> 

> Jurnii 360 gives your commercial team that visibility automatically, every day.
> 

> 
> 

> Could I show you how your current promotional position compares to the market in a 20-minute demo — does [day] work?
> 

**Input required:** Brand Framework Chapter 6 (tone), Chapter 7 (persona messaging), Jurnii 360 proof points.

### 1.2 Named account research brief template 🔴

**What it is:** A one-page structured brief that captures everything needed to personalise outreach to a specific named account before any contact is made.

**Job in the sales process:** Prevents generic outreach. Ensures every message to a Priority 1 or Priority 2 account is grounded in specific knowledge about that company, their market position, their key people, and the angle most likely to resonate.

**What it should contain:**

- Company overview (brands, markets, revenue tier, platform stack)
- Key contacts and their roles (CPO, CCO, CMO — with LinkedIn profiles)
- Known commercial pain (based on public information: recent results, regulatory exposure, competitive moves)
- Which Jurnii product fits best and why
- Whether Jurnii has existing UX data on any of their brands (changes the outreach opener entirely)
- Fraser or partner connection (warm path in, if any)
- Recommended opening angle (what data point or market observation opens the door?)
- Next action and owner

**Format:** Notion database template. One entry per named account. 30 accounts minimum at launch.

**Input required:** Named account list, Jurnii UX brand dataset, Fraser's network knowledge, partner connections.

### 1.3 Market intelligence reports (quarterly) 🔴

**What it is:** A data-led report on a specific iGaming market moment — promotional activity around a major sporting event, UX trends across a vertical, or a regional competitive snapshot — produced quarterly using Jurnii platform data.

**Job in the sales process:** The highest-credibility outreach trigger available. When sent to a named account as the opening message, a market report demonstrates what Jurnii can do — without any product pitch. It generates replies because it's genuinely useful, not because it's promotional. Also used as a PR asset, event leave-behind, and LinkedIn distribution source.

**What it should contain:**

- Methodology note (what was analysed, over what period, which brands)
- 3–5 key findings, each with a specific data point and a one-sentence commercial implication
- Market context (what broader trend does this data connect to?)
- Commercial implications for operators (what should they do?)
- Jurnii CTA — specific, not generic: "If you want to see how your platform compares to this data, book a demo at [jurnii.io](http://jurnii.io)"
- Jurnii branding, designed to dark brand template

**Format:** PDF (print and digital). Also structured for Webflow publication. Under 6 pages.

**Cadence:** Quarterly. Priority events: Cheltenham (Q1), Grand National (Q2), World Cup qualifiers / Euros (Q3), SiGMA / Q4 review (Q4).

**Example report titles:**

- *The UK Promotional Landscape: Cheltenham 2026 — What the Top 10 Operators Offered and What It Cost Them*
- *Registration Flow Benchmarks: Which UK Sportsbooks Are Losing FTDs Before the First Deposit*
- *LATAM Emerging Markets: UX Readiness Scores Across 40 Operators Entering the Region*

**Input required:** Jurnii platform data export, Fraser narrative (2–3 sentences on the key argument), brand template.

### 1.4 Jurnii vs manual monitoring comparison document 🟡

**What it is:** A single-page document that makes the cost-of-inaction case explicit — comparing what manual competitive monitoring costs in time and coverage against what Jurnii delivers.

**Job in the sales process:** Kills the "we do this manually" objection before or during the demo. Also useful as a leave-behind after demos where this objection was raised.

**What it should contain:**

| Dimension | Manual monitoring | Jurnii 360 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scale | Limited to what a team can manually sweep | 300+ brands (UX), full market coverage (360) |
| Speed | Days to weeks between reviews | Near-real-time, daily, instant alerts |
| Time cost | 30+ hours/week of commercial team time | Fixed licence |
| Objectivity | Dependent on who does the review | Structured criteria, consistent scoring |
| Continuity | Gaps between reviews | Always-on, never misses a move |
| Historical record | Not retained systematically | Full longitudinal database from day one |
| Coverage | Whoever the team remembers to check | Every relevant competitor, every day |
- A "what this means in practice" section: what does a team miss in the 4 weeks between manual sweeps? (Use real Jurnii 360 data — e.g., X promotions launched, Y creative changes, Z competitive moves)
- ROI framing: if 30 hours/week of a commercial analyst's time costs £X per year, Jurnii pays for itself at [pricing tier] within [timeframe]

**Format:** Single A4/letter page. Available as PDF and Notion embed. Designed to dark brand or light template.

**Input required:** Jurnii 360 data on a specific market period, client data on manual effort (if available), pricing information.

### 1.5 ICP-specific landing pages (CPO + CCO) 🟡

**What it is:** Two dedicated website landing pages — one for CPO/product audience, one for CCO/commercial audience — that speak directly to each persona's pain and map it to the relevant Jurnii product.

**Job in the sales process:** When outreach drives a prospect to the Jurnii website, a generic homepage doesn't convert at the same rate as a page that speaks their language. Inbound from LinkedIn content and PR also needs a specific landing page to convert.

**CPO page should contain:**

- Headline: *"Benchmark your UX against 300+ competitors — and prioritise your roadmap by revenue impact"*
- Pain: registration flow drop-off, subjective internal debate, slow vendor roadmaps
- Mechanism: Jurnii UX — 70+ commercially weighted recommendations in minutes
- Proof: 300+ brands analysed, case study pull quote, one specific metric
- CTA: *"See how your platform compares"* → demo booking

**CCO page should contain:**

- Headline: *"Know what every competitor is promoting — the moment it goes live"*
- Pain: manual monitoring at scale, being two weeks behind competitive moves, promotional budget without market context
- Mechanism: Jurnii 360 — 21 features, daily updates, real-time alerts
- Proof: 400+ monthly promotions tracked, 30+ hours saved, Promo Richness Index
- CTA: *"See what your competitors launched this week"* → demo booking

**Format:** Webflow CMS page. Mobile-optimised. Demo CTA above the fold.

**Input required:** Brand Framework Chapters 4 and 5, existing case study pull quotes, Webflow CMS access.

## Stage 2 — Demo assets

*Assets used during the 20-minute demo to make the conversation structured, credible, and focused on the prospect's specific situation.*

---

### 2.1 Demo script (structured) 🔴

**What it is:** A structured walkthrough of the standard 20-minute Jurnii demo — not a word-for-word script, but a framework that ensures the same narrative logic is followed in every demo regardless of who delivers it.

**Job in the sales process:** Consistency. The best demos at Jurnii are the ones where the narrative is tight and the product evidence is specific. This document captures that and ensures it's repeatable. Also the foundation for the post-demo follow-up pack.

**What it should contain:**

- Minutes 0–3: Context setting — confirm the prospect's specific pain before showing anything ("Before I show you the product, can I just confirm — is it UX benchmarking, competitive monitoring, or both that's the priority for you today?")
- Minutes 3–8: The market context moment — show the scale of the problem with real data (400+ monthly promos, 70–80% player multi-homing)
- Minutes 8–16: Product demonstration — tailored to their stated priority (Jurnii UX or 360 or both). For each product: show the finding, show the competitive benchmark, show the commercial consequence.
- Minutes 16–19: Proof — one client reference (permitted), one specific outcome
- Minute 19–20: The ask — specific next step. Not "I'll send you some info." "The logical next step is a short pilot on [their market/brand] — does that make sense to explore?"
- Objection responses to have ready: the four most common objections that arise during demos

**Format:** Notion page. Printed one-pager for reference during live calls.

**Input required:** Fraser records a 20-minute Loom of his standard demo. Transcript structured by Claude into this format.

---

### 2.2 Product one-pagers — Jurnii UX and Jurnii 360 🔴

**What it is:** Two single-page product summaries — one per product — that can be shared before, during, or after a demo to anchor the product description in a consistent, designed format.

**Job in the sales process:** Reference document that the prospect can keep, share internally, and use to brief colleagues who weren't in the demo. Also used at events as the primary leave-behind.

**Jurnii UX one-pager should contain:**

- Product positioning line: *"See what your customers see"*
- What it analyses (four categories: Journey Effectiveness, Usability, Performance, Perception)
- What it produces (70+ commercially weighted recommendations, competitive benchmark, roadmap)
- Proof points: 300+ brands, delivered in minutes
- Named clients (where permitted)
- Demo CTA with QR code and URL

**Jurnii 360 one-pager should contain:**

- Product positioning line: *"See what your competitors launch"*
- What it tracks (21 features — summarised in 6 key capability areas)
- What it produces (daily intelligence, real-time alerts, Promo Richness Index, MMM-ready data)
- Proof points: 30+ hours saved/week, 400+ monthly promotions tracked
- Named clients (where permitted)
- Demo CTA with QR code and URL

**Format:** Single-sided A4/letter. Dark brand design. Print-ready PDF and digital version.

**Input required:** Brand Framework Chapter 5 (product messaging), design template, approved client references.

---

### 2.3 Competitive benchmark snapshot (live demo asset) 🟡

**What it is:** A pre-prepared set of competitive benchmark snapshots for 3–5 major UK operators — showing how their UX scores and promotional activity compare — used during demos to demonstrate Jurnii's data coverage before a prospect has signed.

**Job in the sales process:** The single most effective demo moment is when Jurnii shows a prospect data about their competitors that the prospect didn't know. This asset makes that moment repeatable. "Here's what Bet365, Sky Bet, and Paddy Power promoted last week" lands harder than any product description.

**What it should contain:**

- UX benchmark scores for 5 major UK operators across the four Jurnii UX categories
- Promo Richness Index comparison for the same 5 operators over the last 30 days
- One specific market move that happened in the last 7 days (a new promotional mechanic, a homepage redesign, a major offer launch)
- Formatted to show as a live Jurnii platform screen or as a branded PDF

**Format:** Live platform screenshots updated weekly, plus a static PDF version for use when screen-sharing isn't possible. Updated every Monday morning.

**Input required:** Jurnii 360 and UX platform data, weekly refresh cadence.

## Stage 3 — Post-demo assets

*Assets that convert a demo into a pilot commitment — sent within 24 hours of every demo.*

---

### 3.1 Post-demo follow-up pack 🔴

**What it is:** A structured follow-up document assembled and sent within 2 hours of every demo. Personalised to the specific conversation — references the pain points raised, the product shown, and the agreed next step.

**Job in the sales process:** The gap between demo and pilot decision is where most deals are lost. The post-demo pack keeps momentum, provides the internal champion with everything they need to brief colleagues, and makes the next step explicit.

**What it should contain:**

- Opening paragraph: 2–3 sentences that reference the specific conversation ("As we discussed, your main priority is [their stated pain] — here's what Jurnii can do about it specifically")
- Product one-pager (relevant product based on what was shown)
- 1–2 relevant proof points or data screenshots from the demo
- Case study (relevant to their product type — UX or 360)
- Proposed pilot scope: specific brands, specific market, specific duration, specific output
- Pricing overview (if at the right stage)
- One clear next step with a specific date: "I'll follow up on [day] — or if you'd like to move faster, here's a direct link to book the pilot kick-off: [link]"

**Format:** Short personalised email + PDF attachment (or Notion share link). The email is under 150 words. The attached pack is 3–4 pages.

**Input required:** Demo notes (pain points raised, product shown, questions asked, agreed next step), product one-pagers, relevant case study, pricing guide.

---

### 3.2 Case studies — Jurnii UX and Jurnii 360 🔴

**What it is:** Two published case studies — one per product — showing a real client outcome in Situation → Task → Action → Result format. Immediate priority: Flutter or Tombola for UX; Honore Gaming or PowerPlay for 360.

**Job in the sales process:** Kills the "does it actually work?" objection. The most common blocker at the post-demo stage is platform doubt — prospects want to know that another operator went through this and got a specific, measurable outcome. Case studies are the most direct answer to that question.

**What each case study should contain:**

- **Situation:** What was the client's commercial challenge before Jurnii? (Specific — not "they wanted to improve their UX")
- **Task:** What did they need Jurnii to do? Which product, which market, which team?
- **Action:** Specifically what did Jurnii analyse, track, or produce?
- **Result:** What changed commercially? At minimum: time saved, decisions made faster, specific finding that drove a change, any metric improvement where client permits
- One pull quote from a named client contact, signed off before publication
- 400–600 words
- Client logo (where permitted)

**Example UX case study framing:**

> *A Tier 1 UK operator was losing players at the deposit stage without knowing why. Jurnii UX identified 14 friction points in the deposit journey, ranked by conversion impact, that internal teams had not prioritised. Within 8 weeks of implementing the top 5 recommendations, mobile deposit conversion improved by [X]%.*
> 

**Example 360 case study framing:**

> *A multi-brand operator's commercial team was spending 25+ hours per week manually tracking competitor promotions across five UK operators — and still missing moves. Jurnii 360 automated the full monitoring function, cut the team's research time to under 2 hours per week, and surfaced a competitor campaign 72 hours before it launched — enabling a pre-emptive counter-offer.*
> 

**Format:** Web version (Webflow) + PDF version (sales distribution). Dual format always.

**Input required:** Champion client interview transcript (8 questions: before state, Jurnii's role, specific outcomes, time saved, decision made, quote).

---

### 3.3 Pilot proposal template 🟡

**What it is:** A short, structured proposal document that defines the scope, deliverables, timeline, and success criteria for a Jurnii pilot — sent after demo when a prospect is ready to move to a paid trial.

**Job in the sales process:** Removes ambiguity about what a pilot involves. Gives the prospect something concrete to share with procurement, their manager, or their commercial team. Makes the decision easy.

**What it should contain:**

- Proposed pilot scope: which brands, which product (UX or 360 or both), which market
- Duration: typically 4–8 weeks
- What Jurnii will deliver during the pilot (specific outputs: audit reports, 360 dashboard access, competitive snapshots, Promo Richness Index report)
- What success looks like: 2–3 specific success criteria agreed with the prospect before the pilot starts
- What the prospect needs to provide: access, contacts, approvals
- Commercial terms: pilot pricing, what converts to a full contract and on what terms
- Signatures / acceptance section

**Format:** Branded PDF, 2 pages maximum. Fraser reviews every proposal before sending.

**Input required:** Demo notes, agreed pilot scope, current pricing guide.

---

### 3.4 "Not now" nurture email sequence 🟡

**What it is:** A 3-touch email sequence for prospects who have seen a demo but have said "not now" — with a specific reason logged (budget cycle, mid-contract, wrong timing, internal sign-off needed).

**Job in the sales process:** Most Jurnii deals will not close on the first demo. The "not now" cohort is a high-value pipeline asset — they have seen the product and understood the value. The nurture sequence keeps Jurnii visible and relevant until their buying window opens, without being a sales sequence.

**What the three touches should be:**

- **Touch 1 (month 1):** A piece of genuinely useful content — the latest market report, a relevant data point from Jurnii's platform, a piece of press coverage. No pitch. "Thought this might be relevant given what we discussed."
- **Touch 2 (month 2):** A specific market observation that connects to the pain they raised in the demo. "We've been tracking [their market] over the last 4 weeks. [Specific thing happened]. Given what you told me about [their pain], I thought it was worth sharing."
- **Touch 3 (month 3 or at their buying trigger):** The re-engagement ask. "It's been a few months since we spoke — your [contract/budget cycle/sign-off] should be [approaching/resolved]. Worth picking up the conversation? Happy to do a quick refresh on what's changed in the platform."

**Format:** Notion page with three template blocks, each under 100 words. Notes on how to personalise each touch.

**Input required:** [Customer.io](http://Customer.io) tagging ("not now" + reason + date), Brand Framework Chapter 6 (tone).

## Stage 4 — Retention & expansion assets

*Assets that protect existing client relationships and create commercial expansion opportunities.*

---

### 4.1 Monthly client value email template 🟡

**What it is:** A personalised monthly email sent to every active client, showing the most commercially relevant intelligence from their market that Jurnii has captured in the last 30 days.

**Job in the sales process:** Prevents the "we haven't had time to log in" churn pattern. Keeps Jurnii visible and valuable between platform sessions. Makes renewal conversations easier because the client has a 12-month record of value delivered.

**What it should contain:**

- 1–2 specific market moves from their competitive set that Jurnii tracked in the last 30 days
- One UX observation relevant to their product (if they have Jurnii UX)
- One commercial implication: what should they do with this information?
- A link to the platform for the full picture
- Total: under 150 words. Written as if from Fraser personally.

**Format:** Plain text email, sent from Fraser's address. Not a designed newsletter — a personal note.

**Input required:** Client name, product, market, latest platform data for their competitive set. Claude generates per client monthly.

---

### 4.2 Expansion pitch — second product 🟢

**What it is:** A short, specific conversation framework for introducing the second Jurnii product to a client who currently has one (i.e., pitching 360 to a Jurnii UX client, or UX to a 360 client).

**Job in the sales process:** The easiest commercial conversation Jurnii can have — a client who already trusts the platform, already uses it, and needs to be shown the commercial case for adding the second product. This is not a cold pitch — it's a value extension.

**What it should contain:**

- The connection between the two products: "You're already using Jurnii UX to see what your customers see. Jurnii 360 adds the other half — what your competitors are launching."
- One specific data point from the second product that's directly relevant to their market right now
- A proposed 4-week expansion pilot at a reduced rate
- One slide or one-pager showing the combined intelligence layer view

**Format:** 1-page PDF or structured email. Under 200 words. Demo booking link.

**Input required:** Client's current product, their market, relevant 360 or UX data from their competitive set.

## Stage 5 — Events & partner assets

*Assets for in-person commercial contexts and channel partner activation.*

---

### 5.1 Event content pack 🔴

**What it is:** A curated set of materials taken to every industry event — assembled fresh for each event with the most current data and most relevant product content.

**Job in the sales process:** Events are Jurnii's highest-conversion channel. ICE 2025 produced 1 sale and 2 SoWs from a single event. The event content pack ensures every conversation starts from a position of credibility and leaves a tangible asset behind.

**Standard pack contents:**

- Latest quarterly market report (print-ready PDF, branded)
- Jurnii UX one-pager (dark brand, print quality)
- Jurnii 360 one-pager (dark brand, print quality)
- 2 case studies (1 UX, 1 360)
- Demo booking card with QR code → [jurnii.io/demo](http://jurnii.io/demo)
- Partner briefing one-pager (for partner conversations)

**Event-specific additions:**

- A data snapshot relevant to the event's market or theme (e.g., at SBC Malta: a snapshot of LATAM promotional activity from Jurnii 360; at EGR London: a UK UX benchmark comparison)
- Pre-booked meeting list with research brief per contact (built 2 weeks before each event)

**Format:** Print PDF set (A5 or A4 depending on event) + digital versions for email follow-up. Event-specific data snapshot updated per event.

**Input required:** Confirmed event calendar, Jurnii platform data, case studies, product one-pagers, demo booking link.

---

### 5.2 Partner briefing pack 🟡

**What it is:** A 2-page briefing document written for partner types (iGaming agencies, platform providers, consultancies) that explains what Jurnii is, how the partnership works, and how to introduce Jurnii to their clients.

**Job in the sales process:** Partners delivered 5 of Jurnii's first 17 clients. The partner channel is currently informal and unscalable. A briefing pack gives partners something easy to forward, a clear description they can use, and a direct route to a demo for their clients.

**What it should contain:**

- What Jurnii is in one sentence (from the partner's perspective — not a competitor, a complement)
- How Jurnii works alongside the partner's own service (for agencies: augments delivery; for platforms: embedded intelligence; for consultancies: evidence layer for strategy work)
- What clients get: the three-line value prop for operators
- How the introduction works: a demo booking link and a warm intro email template the partner can forward
- Commercial terms of the partnership (referral arrangement, if applicable)

**Three versions needed:**

- Agency version: Jurnii augments UX and CX agency delivery — not a competitor, a data layer
- Platform/tech provider version: Jurnii embeds into the operator's intelligence stack alongside the platform
- Consultancy/advisory version: Jurnii provides the evidence base for strategic recommendations

**Format:** 2-page PDF per version. Light brand design. Personal intro letter from Fraser.

**Input required:** Active partner list, partner type classification, current referral terms, Brand Framework Chapter 5.

---

### 5.3 Speaking abstract template 🟢

**What it is:** A reusable template for conference speaking abstract submissions — structured to Jurnii's standard narrative arc and adaptable to different event themes and markets.

**Job in the sales process:** Conference speaking is a credibility multiplier. A 20-minute talk at SBC Malta or EGR reaches more qualified prospects simultaneously than any outbound sequence. Speaking submissions require abstracts 3–6 months in advance — this template ensures they're submitted consistently and on-brand.

**Standard abstract structure:**

1. The market problem (2 sentences — specific and commercial)
2. Jurnii's data insight on the problem (1–2 sentences — what does our platform data show that the audience doesn't know?)
3. Commercial implication for operators in the room (2 sentences — what should they do?)
4. The talk's takeaway promise (1 sentence — what will the audience leave with?)

**Example abstract (SBC Malta, LATAM focus):**

> *LATAM is the fastest-growing regulated iGaming market — and also the least benchmarked. Operators entering the region are making UX and promotional decisions without any structured data on what "good" looks like for a LATAM player. Jurnii's platform has now analysed 40+ operators across Brazil, Colombia, and Mexico. In this session, we'll share what the data reveals: which UX patterns convert, which promotional mechanics are saturating, and where the competitive gaps are widest. Attendees will leave with a structured framework for evaluating their own LATAM readiness.*
> 

**Format:** Notion template. Under 300 words. Fraser reviews and personalises before every submission.

**Input required:** Event name and theme, Jurnii's most relevant data point for that market, confirmed attendance.
