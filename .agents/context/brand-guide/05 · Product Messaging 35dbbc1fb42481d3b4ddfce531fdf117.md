## Product architecture overview

Jurnii operates as a single intelligence layer built on two complementary products:

**Jurnii UX** answers the question: *How does your experience compare to your competitors and to market best practice?*

**Jurnii 360** answers the question: *What are your competitors doing commercially — right now?*

Together, they give an operator a complete picture of where they stand in the market — both on experience quality and on commercial proposition. They can be purchased independently or together. The combination is the strongest commercial case.

**The emerging execution layer (roadmap):** Jurnii is building toward converting intelligence into automated output — UX concept generation from audit findings, dynamic front-end frameworks informed by continuous competitor tracking, and regional optimisation models. This converts Jurnii from a research layer into a production tool. Roadmap messaging guidance is in the final section of this chapter.

## Jurnii UX — canonical description

### What it is

An AI-powered UX benchmarking platform that analyses any operator's digital experience against a competitive set and market best practice — delivering structured, commercially weighted recommendations in minutes, not weeks.

### What it does

Jurnii UX runs a deep structural analysis across four categories:

1. **Journey Effectiveness** — how effectively the product guides users toward their specific goals across all key journeys (registration, deposit, betting, withdrawal, account management)
2. **Usability** — how easily users can navigate, understand, and use the product through deep heuristic analysis
3. **Performance** — the technical quality of the front-end experience: page load, SEO, accessibility
4. **Perception** — how the product experience shapes how users feel about the brand

Across these four categories, Jurnii UX identifies 70+ commercially weighted recommendations per audit. Each recommendation is categorised, ranked by severity, and mapped to a specific journey and commercial consequence.

### What it produces

- 70+ ranked recommendations per audit, commercially weighted and mapped to revenue impact
- Full competitive benchmarking — your score vs your competitive set
- Roadmap of UX optimisations ready for immediate prioritisation
- Delivered in minutes via the platform

### Positioning statement

**"See what your customers see."**

### Key proof points

- 300+ brands analysed
- 4 analysis categories, 70+ recommendations per audit
- Delivered in minutes (not weeks like agency alternatives)
- Commercially weighted — every finding mapped to revenue consequence
- Regional nuance built in — benchmarks calibrated for UK, EU, LATAM, and North America

### Who buys it

Primary: CPO, Head of UX/CX

Secondary: CEO (strategic view), CMO (brand perception category)

## Jurnii 360 — canonical description

### What it is

An automated competitor proposition intelligence platform that tracks, catalogues, and analyses competitor promotional activity, banner creative, messaging, and commercial strategy — updated daily, with real-time alerts for significant moves.

### What it does

Jurnii 360 captures and structures competitor commercial activity across 21 feature areas, including:

- **Competitor promotion tracking** — all live promotions across sportsbook and casino, including mechanics, creative execution, and targeting
- **Release timing insights** — when and how often competitors launch promotions, daily boosts, and event-based offers
- **Offer benchmarking** — bonus size, odds boosts, free bet value, duration, and targeting vs the competitive set
- **Banner creative & messaging intelligence** — homepage banners, promotional sliders, on-site messaging, and creative approaches over time
- **Market segmentation & targeting analysis** — which player segments competitors are targeting with specific promotions
- **Trend spotting & market shifts** — emerging promotional trends across the industry before they become standard
- **Real-time alerts** — instant notifications when competitors launch a major offer or change strategy
- **Historical database & reporting** — full promotional history for longitudinal trend analysis and competitive playbook identification
- **Promo Richness Index** — Jurnii's proprietary metric quantifying promotional generosity across all operators, enabling "we are 23% less generous than Bet365 on horse racing" level commercial conversations
- **MMM-ready data export** — pre-formatted competitive promotional data that feeds directly into Marketing Mix Modelling as market context variables

### What it produces

- Daily updated competitive intelligence across 21 feature areas
- Real-time alerts on competitor moves
- Board-ready metrics (Promo Richness Index)
- MMM-ready competitive data exports
- 30+ hours saved per week per commercial team

### Positioning statement

**"See what your competitors launch."**

### Key proof points

- 21 features, daily updates, real-time intelligence
- 30+ hours saved per week
- Hours not days to react
- 100% of competitor moves tracked automatically
- MMM-ready data for marketing mix modelling
- Promo Richness Index — a proprietary, defensible competitive metric

### Who buys it

Primary: CCO, CMO, Head of CRM/Retention

Secondary: CEO (strategic intelligence), VP Trading (odds and boost context)

## Roadmap messaging

Jurnii's product roadmap includes capabilities that are in development and not yet live. This section defines the approved language for discussing what's coming — without overpromising.

**Automated UX concept generation**

Approved framing: "Jurnii is building toward automated prototype generation — taking a high-impact UX finding and generating an optimised design concept in your brand's UI, ready to test or implement. This converts Jurnii from a research layer into a production tool."

**Dynamic front-end framework**

Approved framing: "The longer-term vision is a modular UI framework that evolves continuously as the market shifts — informed by Jurnii's real-world competitor tracking and regional performance data."

**Multi-region and multi-brand dashboards**

Approved framing: "Multi-region and multi-brand dashboard views are on the near-term roadmap — enabling group-level operators to manage intelligence across their full portfolio in a single view."

**What not to say:** Do not present roadmap items as current functionality. Do not give delivery timelines unless confirmed by the product team. Do not promise features in commercial negotiations without sign-off.
