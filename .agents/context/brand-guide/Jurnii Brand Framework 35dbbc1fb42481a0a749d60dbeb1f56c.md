# Jurnii Brand Framework

> This is the single source of truth for how Jurnii communicates — who we are, what we stand for, how we sound, and what we say. It is used by every employee and loaded as context for all Claude tasks. No content should be created without referencing this document first.
> 

---

## How to use this document

This framework is structured in ten chapters, each covering a distinct layer of the brand. Read chapters 1–3 first — they form the strategic foundation everything else builds on. Chapters 4–7 are the working reference for day-to-day communication. Chapters 8–10 cover visual identity, channel standards, and measurement.

**For employees:** Use this as your reference before writing anything externally — outreach, content, event materials, or press responses.

**For Claude tasks:** The minimum context load for any content task is: Chapter 1 (purpose + values), Chapter 3 (relevant narrative pillar), Chapter 4 (target ICP and role), Chapter 5 (relevant product), and Chapter 6 (tone of voice + vocabulary). For sales tasks, also load Chapter 7.
