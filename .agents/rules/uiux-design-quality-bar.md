# UI/UX Design Quality Bar

This rule applies to all UI/UX design sprints, page upgrades, and template refactoring within the Jurnii web properties. It establishes a hard floor for visual quality.

## The Mandates

1. **Do not treat design as cosmetic:** Design must support commercial logic, build enterprise credibility, and drive conversions. It is not about making things "pretty."
2. **Do not implement before critique:** No CSS or HTML changes are allowed until a design concept and architecture plan have been reviewed and approved by the Principal UI/UX Designer and Design Systems Architect.
3. **Do not use generic SaaS patterns:** Standard 3-column white card grids on light-grey backgrounds are banned unless significantly adapted to fit Jurnii's data-forward, premium aesthetic.
4. **Do not use card grids as the default answer:** If you can express an idea through a single, high-fidelity intelligence panel or a split-screen problem/solution layout, do that instead of defaulting to a grid of cards.
5. **Do not add icons unless they clarify meaning:** Lucide icons should be used for UI wayfinding or data categorization, not as decorative filler in large circles.
6. **Do not overuse green:** Brand Green (`#94ff96`) is powerful. Use it sparingly for primary CTAs, critical data highlights, and strategic accents. Overuse dilutes its impact.
7. **Do not accept "better spacing" as sufficient:** A design sprint must result in a stronger *concept*, not just cleaner margins. 
8. **Every page needs a visual concept:** There must be a focal point (a dashboard mockup, a diagnostic graph, a structured data panel) that anchors the page.
9. **Every template needs a reusable design system:** Classes must be abstracted into `site.css`. No inline styles.
10. **Browser review CAN and WILL reject implementation:** The Browser Design Critic subagent is empowered to reject work that looks sloppy, broken on mobile, or visually weak.
11. **Final creative review is required:** A sprint is not complete until the Creative Director Review Board skill approves the final output.
