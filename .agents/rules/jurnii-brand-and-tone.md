# Rule: Jurnii Brand and Tone

## Description
Enforces the specific "calm authority" tone of the Jurnii brand.

## Directives
1. **Calm Authority:** Write with the tone of a senior commercial strategist. Be confident without being loud.
2. **Commercial Precision:** Use specific, commercially literate language (NGR, CAC, LTV, churn, conversion). Avoid UX softness ("delightful", "seamless").
3. **Data-backed Claims:** Use specific numbers. Do not use unsupported superlatives.
4. **No AI Hype:** Never use words like "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "next-generation." Do not use "AI-powered" alone without specifying the mechanism.
5. **No Consulting Filler:** Avoid words like "leverage," "synergies," "holistic," "robust."
6. **Sentence Structure:** Use short sentences under pressure. One idea per sentence where possible. Active voice, present tense.
7. **CTAs:** Ensure a specific CTA exists on every commercial page (e.g., "Book a Demo", "See how your platform compares").
