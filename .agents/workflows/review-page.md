# Workflow: Review Page

**Purpose**: Review a redesigned page to ensure it meets quality and commercial ontology standards.

Check the following areas on the redesigned page:

- **Visual Hierarchy**: Does the layout guide the eye to the most important elements?
- **Copy Clarity**: Is the messaging concise, clear, and aligned with the ontology?
- **Ontology Consistency**: Ensure there is no category confusion between product, solution, persona, and use case.
- **CTA Clarity**: Are the primary and secondary calls-to-action distinct and compelling?
- **Mobile Responsiveness**: Does the page break or degrade on smaller screen sizes?
- **Accessibility**: Are basic accessibility standards (e.g., alt text, contrast, aria-labels) met?
- **SEO Basics**: Are title tags, meta descriptions, and heading hierarchies correct?
- **Link Integrity**: Are there any broken internal or external links?
- **Analytics/CRM Implications**: Are necessary tracking hooks or IDs in place for CRM alignment?
