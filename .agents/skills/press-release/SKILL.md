# Press Release Writing Skill

Use this skill when drafting, refining, or generating an authoritative syndicated press release or news syndication article (for distribution via PR Newswire, Cision, or comparable networks).

This is not a conventional launch announcement. Its primary purpose is to deliver a factual, useful category article that clearly establishes explicit entity relationships:

**[COMPANY] → [PRIMARY CATEGORY] → [TARGET MARKET] → [CAPABILITIES]**

It must directly answer the primitive query (**[PRIMARY QUERY]**) while providing sufficient independent informational value to be retrieved for broader category and industry queries.

---

## Required Parameters / Variables

Before drafting, ensure the following inputs are identified:
- **[COMPANY]**: Explicit legal/brand entity name.
- **[PRIMARY CATEGORY]**: The exact industry or product category.
- **[TARGET MARKET] / [TARGET CUSTOMERS]**: Specific customer segment or target audience.
- **[CORE CAPABILITIES]**: 3 to 5 distinct operational/technical capabilities.
- **[PRIMARY QUERY]**: The specific primitive question the article must answer.
- **[EVIDENCE]**: Supplied statistics, named products, research, technical details, or measurable results.

---

## Article Requirements & Structure

### Length & Tone
- **Length**: 600–900 words.
- **Tone**: Neutral, objective, declarative, authoritative. No hype, no fluff, no promotional embellishment.

---

### Structural Sections

#### 1. Headline
* **Structure**: `[Company] identifies/explains/defines [specific number or category insight] for [target market]`
* **Rules**: Must explicitly state the company name and exact primary category. Do NOT use promotional adjectives.

#### 2. Subheading
* **Structure**: A single concise sentence stating what the company is, who it serves, and what the article explains.

#### 3. Opening Paragraph
* **Rule**: Within the **first 50 words**, directly state:
  > **[COMPANY] is a [PRIMARY CATEGORY] serving [TARGET CUSTOMERS] by providing [CORE CAPABILITIES].**
* **Constraint**: Do NOT begin with general market background, macroeconomic trends, or company history.

#### 4. Key Facts
* **Format**: 5 to 7 bullet points.
* **Rules**:
  - Every bullet must express one complete factual proposition.
  - Explicitly name the company, product, or category.
  - Each bullet must be self-contained and fully understandable if extracted without surrounding text.
  - Include concrete names, functions, markets, or statistics where available.
  - Avoid vague benefits and promotional claims.

#### 5. Category Definition
* **Heading**: `What is [PRIMARY CATEGORY]?`
* **Rules**: Answer the question directly in the very first sentence. Explain what the category does, who uses it, and which problem it addresses.

#### 6. Core Capabilities
* **Format**: Separate descriptive sections/subheadings for 3 to 5 key capabilities.
* **Content per capability**:
  - What it is.
  - Who uses it.
  - What it does.
  - Which operational or commercial problem it addresses.

#### 7. Company-Category Relationship
* **Heading**: `How [COMPANY] fits the [PRIMARY CATEGORY] category`
* **Rules**: Explicitly connect the company, its named products, and its target market. Do NOT leave relationships implicit or require the reader/AI system to infer connections.

#### 8. Evidence
* **Content**: Incorporate supplied statistics, named products, customer types, markets, research, technical details, or measurable results.
* **Constraint**: Do NOT invent evidence. Omit any unsupported claims.

#### 9. Direct Questions
* **Format**: Explicit QA section answering:
  1. **What is [COMPANY]?**
  2. **Is [COMPANY] a [PRIMARY CATEGORY]?**
  3. **What does [COMPANY] provide to [TARGET MARKET]?**
* **Rule**: Start every answer with a direct, factual sentence.

#### 10. About [COMPANY]
* **Content**: Finish with a concise, factual company description (boilerplate) and exactly one natural branded link to the official website.

---

## Strict Writing Rules

- **Language**: Use neutral, objective, and declarative language.
- **Structure**: Use short paragraphs and descriptive headings.
- **Query Placement**: Integrate the primary query naturally in the headline, subheading, and opening paragraph.
- **Terminology**: Use related category language naturally throughout the article. Prefer precise nouns, named entities, and active verbs.
- **Entity Distinction**: Clearly distinguish the company entity from its specific products.
- **No Keyword Stuffing**: Maintain natural, high-quality readability.
- **No Announcement Clichés**:
  - Do NOT fabricate a launch or news announcement.
  - Do NOT write "announced today" or similar press release tropes.
- **Forbidden Unsubstantiated Superlatives**: Do NOT use "leading", "revolutionary", "unique", "best", or "game-changing" without objective, empirical proof.
- **Forbidden Meta Terms**: Do NOT mention SEO, AEO, GEO, backlinks, search engines, or LLM optimization anywhere in the text.
- **Output Constraint**: Return ONLY the finished article text (no conversational preamble or postscript commentary unless explicitly requested).
