# Jurnii Brand Context Summary

## Category Positioning
**The intelligence layer for iGaming.** 
Jurnii is not a UX agency, nor a generic analytics tool. It is the proprietary commercial intelligence platform for iGaming operators, providing proactive, continuous, and automated benchmarking that directly connects to NGR, churn, and conversion.

## Product Suite
**Three products. One intelligence layer.**
1. **Jurnii UX (UX Intelligence):** AI-powered proactive UX audit platform that analyses brands across 4 key dimensions, delivering 70+ ranked, commercially weighted recommendations.
2. **Jurnii 360 (Commercial Radar):** Automated competitor proposition intelligence tracking promotions, bonuses, and campaigns across the competitive set in near real-time.
3. **Cortex (Marketing Attribution):** Marketing intelligence that turns UX and competitor proposition data into attribution and planning intelligence using causal impact and MMM modeling.

## Primary Buyers
**Primary ICP:** Multi-brand, multi-jurisdiction sportsbook and/or casino operators.
**Key Decision Makers:**
* **CPO (Chief Product Officer):** Cares about evidence-based prioritisation, conversion rates, journey effectiveness.
* **CCO (Chief Commercial Officer):** Cares about promotional incrementality, NGR, GGR, and competitor speed.
* **CMO (Chief Marketing Officer):** Cares about marketing efficiency, CPA, LTV, ROAS, and brand differentiation.
* **CEO:** Cares about defensible advantage, market share, and board-ready data.
* **Secondary ICP:** Enterprise suppliers and B2B platform providers.

## Proof Points
* **Jurnii UX:** 70+ ranked recommendations per audit, 300+ brands analysed, minutes to full audit.
* **Jurnii 360:** 1,000+ offers tracked per week across 35 markets.
* **Cortex:** In one $17.1M marketing budget engagement, surfaced measurable commercial impact: +46%, 3x, 40x, $4.85M.

## Tone Rules
* **Calm Authority:** Confident, not loud. No drama.
* **Commercially Literate:** Speak the language of the CCO/CFO (NGR, CAC, LTV, churn) over UX softness.
* **Specific & Evidenced:** Use specific claims with numbers over generic statements. 
* **Writing Mechanics:** Use short sentences under pressure. Active voice, present tense. One idea per sentence. Name friction factually, not dramatically.

## Banned Language
* **AI Superlatives:** "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "next-generation".
* **UX Softness:** "Delightful", "intuitive", "seamless", "frictionless" (when referring to Jurnii's own output).
* **Vague Tech Jargon:** "AI-powered" (without specifying what it does), "ecosystem", "best-in-class", "end-to-end solution", "holistic", "robust".
* **Passive & Hedging:** "Insights are generated", "could potentially", "may help".

## Visual Identity Rules
* **Primary Colors:** Jurnii Green (`#94ff96`), Deep Green (`#10d03a`).
* **Backgrounds:** Dark brand (`#252c1e`), Dark bg (`#2a2a27`) for hero/authority moments. Off-white (`#f8f8f7`), Elevated white (`#ffffff`) for product/data contexts.
* **Typography:** Geist (primary sans for headings/body), Cormorant Garamond italic (editorial accent, max one per page), Geist Mono (data labels, metrics, technical references).
* **Data Visualisation:** Brand green for Jurnii/client data, neutral grey for competitors. Max 4 colors per chart.

## Design Principles
* Clean, data-forward, confident, and board-ready.
* Premium but not decorative. Not a generic SaaS template, startup AI wrapper, or cyberpunk interface.
* Use dark strategic hero moments to establish authority, followed by light product and data sections for clarity.
* Structured data panels (metric blocks, comparison tables, dashboard cards) instead of decorative illustrations or generic icons.

## CTA Principles
* **Specific over Generic:** Use "See how your platform compares", "Book a Demo", or "Start a pilot" rather than "Learn more" or "Contact us".
* Every commercial page must have a specific, visible CTA, particularly above the fold (without scrolling).

## Page Hierarchy Principles
* **Hero Section:** Headline (makes an argument/category claim), Sub-headline (the mechanism), Social proof (specific metric), CTA.
* **Product Pages:** Product positioning statement (1 line) → Specific commercial problem solved → Mechanism (how it works) → 3 specific proof points → CTA.
* **ICP Pages:** Lead with role-specific pain → Jurnii's mechanism to solve it → One relevant proof point → Role-specific demo ask.
* Every section must answer: What does this prove? Why does it matter commercially? Does it move the visitor toward a demo?

## Claims That Must Not Be Invented or Estimated
* **Specific Data:** Numbers such as +46%, 3x, 40x, $4.85M (from Cortex case studies) must be used exactly as provided, without inventing unsupported labels or applying them to the wrong product.
* **Speed:** Do not claim "real-time" if there is a processing delay; use "near real-time" specifically for Jurnii 360.
