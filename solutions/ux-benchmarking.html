How We Benchmark

Jurnii breaks down the subjective concept of 'good UX' into measurable, objective dimensions ranked by commercial impact.

Journeys

Quantifying path-to-conversion and friction density across all player flows. We identify exact drop-off points in your betslip and market navigation.

Usability

Heuristic evaluation of interface clarity, error recovery, and interaction design against top market leaders.

Performance

Objective measurement of load times, responsiveness, and technical stability.

Perception

Brand trust, visual hierarchy, and the subjective feel of the commercial environment. We quantify how your product experience shapes player loyalty.
