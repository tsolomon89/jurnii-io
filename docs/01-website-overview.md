# 01 — Website Overview

This website explains **Jurnii**, the intelligence layer for modern iGaming operators. The site is built to establish market authority, educate operators on Jurnii's products, and drive interested buyers toward booking a pilot or a demo.

## Who This Site is For
The primary target readers are executive decision-makers at multi-brand, multi-jurisdiction sportsbooks and casino operators.
* **Chief Product Officers (CPOs):** Looking for evidence-based product prioritization.
* **Chief Commercial Officers (CCOs):** Looking to protect net gaming revenue (NGR) and match competitor offerings.
* **Chief Marketing Officers (CMOs):** Looking for marketing budget attribution and lower customer acquisition costs (CAC).
* **Chief Executive Officers (CEOs):** Looking for defensible market advantage and board-ready growth reports.

## Main Conversion Path
The main goal of every page is conversion. The visitor journey points directly to the book-a-demo call to action:
* **The Call to Action (CTA):** Prominently placed in the top navigation, within hero sections, and at the bottom of pages, linking directly to a pilot email path (`mailto:fraser@jurnii.io`).

## Website Area Structure

| Site Area | What it does | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Homepage** | Sets the category position, presents the industry challenge, and hosts an interactive competitor benchmarking tool. | Establishes immediate authority and lets visitors interact with Jurnii's data. |
| **Product Pages** | Highlights the three core products: Jurnii UX, Jurnii 360, and Cortex. | Explains the mechanisms and commercial value of each specific product offering. |
| **Feature Pages** | Provides deep details on atomic capabilities like promotions tracking, live feeds, usability heuristics, and market trends. | Proves that Jurnii has a deep, functional data collection network behind its products. |
| **Solution Pages** | Focuses on high-level operational solutions such as UX benchmarking, competitor pricing, ROI attribution, and churn optimization. | Maps Jurnii's features directly to the business outcomes that leadership teams care about. |
| **Use Cases** | Custom landing areas for specific roles (e.g., CPO, CCO, CMO), company sizes, departments, and industry sectors. | Personalizes the value proposition so each buyer sees how Jurnii fits their specific day-to-day pain. |
| **Demo Path** | Direct email channels for pilot requests. | Connects interested prospects immediately with sales leadership to close the conversion loop. |
