The Commercial Problem

Commercial teams operate in a blind spot. Manually tracking 400+ monthly promotions and 5,000+ odds boosts across competitors is impossible. This results in unexplainable CPA spikes, overspending on uncompetitive mechanics, and reacting days too late to market shifts.

How Jurnii 360 Solves This

Jurnii 360 automates competitor intelligence across 21 feature areas, capturing 100% of competitor moves automatically. It turns unstructured market activity into board-ready metrics like the Promo Richness Index.
