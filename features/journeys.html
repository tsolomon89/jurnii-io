The Commercial Problem

High drop-off rates in critical flows—like registration, initial deposit, and withdrawal—are often invisible to traditional analytics.

You know the conversion rate dropped, but you don't know the specific structural friction causing players to abandon the journey. This blind spot forces product teams to guess rather than optimize.

What analytics misses

Standard product analytics will tell you that 40% of users drop off at registration step 2. It will not tell you that your address lookup API is failing for users in specific postcodes.

How Jurnii Solves This

By auditing the exact step-by-step journeys against market best practice, Jurnii maps specific UX flaws directly to funnel leakage. This allows you to fix the flow before it permanently damages CAC.
