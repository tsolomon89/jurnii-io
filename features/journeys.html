The Commercial Problem

High drop-off rates in critical flows—like registration, initial deposit, and withdrawal—are often invisible to traditional analytics. You know the conversion rate dropped, but you don't know the specific structural friction causing players to abandon the journey.

How Jurnii Solves This

By auditing the exact step-by-step journeys against market best practice, Jurnii maps specific UX flaws directly to funnel leakage. This allows you to fix the flow before it permanently damages CAC.

Friction Identification

Pinpoint the exact field, button, or logic jump causing users to stall during registration.

Competitor Benchmarking

Compare your flow efficiency (steps to bet) against the top 300+ brands in the market.

Revenue Mapping

Translate journey friction into a commercially weighted priority score for your product roadmap.
