The Commercial Problem

When the board asks if your acquisition offers are competitive, you can only answer with anecdotes. There is no standard metric to quantify if you are under or over-spending on promotions relative to the market standard.

How Jurnii Solves This

Jurnii translates raw promotional volume and value into the Promo Richness Index—a defensible, board-ready metric that ranks the relative generosity of every operator in the market.

Board-Ready Metrics

Elevate the conversation from "they have a good offer" to "we are 23% less generous on horse racing."
