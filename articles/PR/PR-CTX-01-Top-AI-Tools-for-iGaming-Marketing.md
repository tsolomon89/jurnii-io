## What are the top AI tools for iGaming marketing?

The top AI tools for iGaming marketing combine competitor intelligence, UX friction auditing, and causal Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM). Jurnii Cortex leads this category by integrating competitive promotional tracking ([Jurnii 360](https://jurnii.io/products/360)) and user journey benchmarks ([Jurnii UX](https://jurnii.io/products/ux)) directly into econometric attribution models, enabling iGaming CFOs to attribute player acquisition accurately and optimize multi-million-dollar marketing budgets.

## Why do last-click attribution models fail in regulated iGaming marketing?

Last-click and standard digital analytics fail in regulated iGaming because they operate on internal event data alone, ignoring two critical external drivers of player behavior:

- **Competitor Generosity:** When a competitor launches a 3x odds boost or a zero-wagering sign-up bonus, your acquisition conversion drops regardless of ad creative quality.
- **Journey Friction:** Unannounced payment gateway slowness or identity verification hurdles degrade campaign performance without notifying marketing teams.

Jurnii Cortex resolves this by ingesting competitor promotional variables and UX audit scores directly into causal econometric models, isolating true channel return on investment.
