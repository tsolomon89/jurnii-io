## How AI Optimization Is Replacing Guesswork in iGaming Promotional Strategy

### Jurnii's proprietary Promo Richness Index quantifies competitive generosity across 35 markets to protect operator margins while maintaining acquisition velocity.

**LONDON, UK — July 23, 2026** — Artificial intelligence is replacing historical guesswork in iGaming promotional strategy by converting unstructured competitive data into quantitative optimization signals.

Historically, iGaming marketing teams set promotional budgets, free bet values, and bonus terms by matching competitor offers or repeating seasonal playbooks. This reactive approach created margin leakage, as operators over-promoted without understanding market-wide generosity baselines or promotional density.

[Jurnii](https://jurnii.io), the commercial intelligence layer for iGaming, resolves this optimization gap through [Jurnii 360](https://jurnii.io/products/360) and its proprietary **Promo Richness Index**. The index converts competitor promotional data into a structured generosity score, enabling marketing and commercial leaders to benchmark offers against objective market evidence.

Jurnii 360 provides three core optimization mechanisms for iGaming CMOs:

1. **Generosity calibration:** Benchmark free bets, deposit match percentages, and odds boosts against market baselines to prevent over-subsidising bonus offers.
2. **Promotional density mapping:** Track offer frequency across specific sports and casino categories to identify underserved promotional timing windows.
3. **MMM-ready data exports:** Stream pre-formatted competitive promotional variables directly into Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM) architectures to isolate promotional uplift from media channel performance.

"A bigger boost is not always better for the operator or the bettor," said Mitch Vidler, Chief Commercial Officer at Jurnii. "Without objective market context, operators over-promote during high-traffic events and erode gross margin. The Promo Richness Index gives CMOs the market evidence required to optimise promotional spend—protecting margins while maintaining competitive acquisition strength across 35 regulated markets."

### Promo Richness Index Optimization Levers

By replacing subjective guesswork with structured competitive data, the Promo Richness Index delivers three concrete commercial improvements:

- **Generosity Calibration:** Rectifies over-subsidised bonus offers by establishing baseline market generosity, protecting gross operational margins.
- **Density Mapping:** Identifies cluttered promotional windows across sports and casino products, driving higher conversion during quiet competitor windows.
- **MMM Integration:** Corrects misattributed channel efficiency by feeding competitive promotional variables into econometric marketing models, ensuring accurately scaled CAC.

## How does AI optimize iGaming promotional strategy?

AI optimizes iGaming promotional strategy by analyzing competitor bonus structures, odds boosts, and promotional frequency in near-real-time. Using quantitative metrics like Jurnii's Promo Richness Index, AI enables operators to calibrate promotional generosity, map market density, and export structured competitive data into Marketing Mix Modelling (MMM) systems, ensuring promotional budgets protect margin while maximizing acquisition.
