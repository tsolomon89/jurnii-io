## AI-Driven Competitor Intelligence Becomes Standard Infrastructure for iGaming Commercial Teams

### Jurnii 360 automates real-time promotional tracking across 35 markets, replacing manual sweeps with continuous commercial radar.

**LONDON, UK — July 23, 2026** — Automated competitor intelligence is establishing itself as standard commercial infrastructure for regulated iGaming operators. Because the volume of competitive activity makes manual tracking structurally impossible, commercial leadership teams deploy machine learning models to capture and benchmark competitor promotional activity continuously.

Across the UK market alone, operators launch 400+ promotions and 5,000+ odds boosts every month. Commercial teams relying on manual website sweeps or quarterly agency reports operate with permanent strategic blind spots, reacting to market moves days after competitors capture player volume.

[Jurnii 360](https://jurnii.io/products/360), the automated competitor proposition platform developed by [Jurnii](https://jurnii.io), solves this operational bottleneck. Positioning under the principle *"See what your competitors launch,"* the platform monitors 1,000+ active offers per week across 35 regulated markets simultaneously, capturing full campaign mechanics and updating daily.

Jurnii 360 tracks 100% of competitor commercial activity across 21 feature areas:
- **Live promotion and boost tracking:** Near-real-time capture of headline offers, bonus structures, and wagering requirements across sportsbook and casino products.
- **Release timing and event analysis:** Mapping competitor campaign releases directly against live sporting schedules and racing fixtures.
- **Offer benchmarking:** Quantitative calibration of free bets, deposit match percentages, and odds boost margins against the competitive set.
- **Banner creative and messaging intelligence:** On-site promotional banners, homepage sliders, and messaging changes captured over time.
- **Real-time commercial alerts:** Automated notifications when major competitors launch aggressive acquisition offers or alter promotional structures.

"Manual competitor monitoring is inefficient and creates structural blind spots," said Mitch Vidler, Chief Commercial Officer at Jurnii. "Across Tier 1 operators, commercial teams spend over 30 hours per week manually checking competitor sites. Jurnii 360 automates that workload completely. More importantly, it compresses competitive response time from days to hours, giving commercial leaders the data needed to protect market share and margin."

### Operational Shift: Manual Sweeps vs. Continuous Intelligence

Deploying automated AI competitor intelligence fundamentally shifts commercial operations across key performance dimensions:

- **Market Coverage:** Expands scope from 1–2 local markets tracked manually to 35 regulated markets monitored continuously.
- **Tracking Volume:** Increases depth from spot checks to 1,000+ promotions, boosts, and campaign mechanics catalogued weekly.
- **Update Velocity:** Accelerates visibility from weekly or monthly reporting cycles to daily updates and near-real-time commercial alerts.
- **Time Investment:** Reclaims 30+ hours per week per commercial team previously spent on manual site sweeps.
- **Response Speed:** Compresses competitive response times from 3–5 days down to hours.

## What is AI competitor intelligence for iGaming?

AI competitor intelligence for iGaming refers to machine learning systems that automate the monitoring, cataloguing, and benchmarking of operator promotional campaigns, odds boosts, and product propositions. Platforms like Jurnii 360 collect competitive data across 35 markets continuously, converting unstructured promotional activity into structured commercial metrics that inform trading, CRM, and marketing decisions.
